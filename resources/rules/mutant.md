Primary Mutation
Choose a primary mutation, which represents the main features granted by your anomalous nature. You can choose between the Aquatic, Bestial, Monstrous, and Venomous primary mutations, each of which is detailed at the end of the class description. Your choice grants you features when you choose it at 1st level and again at 6th, 10th, 14th, and 18th levels.
Secondary Mutations
As you grow in power and experience additional hardships, your form continues to mutate, granting you additional features and adaptations that can help you in your life as an adventurer. At 2nd level, you gain two secondary mutations of your choice. Your secondary mutation options are detailed at the end of the class description. When you gain certain Mutant levels, you gain additional secondary mutations of your choice, as shown in the Secondary Mutations column of the Mutant table. If a Secondary Mutation requires a creature to make a saving throw, the DC is 8 + your Constitution modifier + your proficiency bonus. Additionally, when you gain a level in this class, you can choose one of the mutations you have gained and replace it with another mutation that you could gain at that level. If a secondary mutation has prerequisites, you must meet them to learn it. You can gain the mutation at the same time that you meet its prerequisites. A level prerequisite refers to your level in this class.
Reserves of Strength
At 3rd level, your mutation allows you to call on reserves of strength that others would need time to summon. Hit dice expended by a Reserves of Strength ability cannot be used to recover hit points during a short rest until they are recovered over the course of a long rest, as normal. You gain the following Reserves of Strength options:
Steady Arm When you miss with a weapon attack roll, you can use your reaction to expend a hit die and roll it. Add the result to the attack roll, possibly turning the miss into a hit.
Steady Nerves When you fail an ability check in which you are proficient, you can use your reaction to expend a hit die and roll it. Add the result to the check, possibly turning the failure into a success. Once this ability has turned a failure into a success, you cannot use it again until you finish a long rest.
Ability Score Improvement
When you reach 4th level, and again at 8th, 12th, 16th, and 19th level, you can increase one ability score of your choice by 2, or you can increase two ability scores of your choice by 1. As normal, you can’t increase an ability score above 20 using this feature.
Extra Attack
Starting at 5th level, you can attack twice, instead of once, whenever you take the Attack action on your turn.
Improved Reserves of Strength
By 7th level, you are able to summon your Reserves of Strength in even more situations. You gain the following Reserves of Strength options:
Burst of Power When you score a critical hit with a weapon attack, you can expend a hit die and roll it, adding twice the result to the damage of the critical hit.
Power Through When you take damage, you can use your reaction to roll a hit die, subtracting the result from the damage taken. Perform the subtraction before applying any resistances or vulnerabilities.
Adapted Resistance
At 9th level, you adapt to overcome a common detriment. Choose one of the following conditions: blinded, charmed, frightened, paralyzed, poisoned, or stunned. You have advantage on saving throws against effects that inflict your chosen condition, as well as saving throws to end that condition on you. Each time you gain a level in this class, you can replace the chosen condition with another from this list.
Adrenaline Rush
By 11th level, you are able to summon additional power whenever you call on your Reserves of Strength. Whenever you use any Reserves of Strength option, you have advantage on the first attack roll you make before the end of your next turn.
Resilient Adaptation
At 13th level, your mutation has allowed you to adapt to the dangers that you have faced. You gain proficiency in one saving throw of your choice.
Greater Reserves of Strength
By 15th level, you are able to summon your reserves of strength in even more circumstances. You gain the following Reserves of Strength option:
Rapid Adaptation When you fail a saving throw in which you are proficient, you can use your reaction to expend a hit die and roll it. Add the result to the saving throw, possibly turning the failure into a success.
Deep Reserves
At 17th level, your reserves of strength last longer and carry you farther. When you use any Reserves of Strength option and roll a 1 or a 2 on the hit die, you gain the benefit of the Reserves of Strength option, but the hit die is not expended.
Controlled Evolution
At 20th level, you can take control of your mutation to enhance aspects of yourself while casting off features you no longer need. Choose one of your ability scores. That ability score increases by 4, as does the maximum for that score. Then choose a different ability score. That ability score is increased by 2, as is the maximum for that ability score. Finally, choose one more ability score, different from the first two. That ability score is decreased by 2, as is the maximum for that score.

Primary Mutations
Alchemical
Alchemical Nature
At 1st level, your mutation is a product of your alchemy. Any Mutant class ability or Secondary Mutation ability you gain uses Intelligence, instead of Constitution, to determine its saving throw DC, number of temporary hit points gained, or any other property of the ability normally dependent on Constitution.
Alchemical Warrior
At 1st level, you gain proficiency with the Craft (Alchemy) Skill and martial weapons. In addition, when you gain the Secondary Mutations feature at level 2, you can choose to learn a Fighting Style from the Fighter class in place of one of your Secondary Mutations. You can only gain one Fighting Style in this way, and it has no effect if you have gained that Fighting Style from another source. You can also choose to gain proficiency with heavy armor in place of another one of your Secondary Mutations.
Mutagens
Also at 1st level, you can use alchemically crafted mutagens to enhance your skills. As a bonus action, you can consume a mutagen, which grants you one of the following abilities for one hour:
Endurance While in combat, you gain temporary hit points equal to your Intelligence modifier (minimum 1) at the start of each of your turns. Any remaining temporary hit points vanish when combat ends.
Enhanced Reflexes You can add your Intelligence modifier to Dexterity checks and saving throws.
Enhanced Senses You can add your Intelligence modifier to Wisdom (Perception) and Wisdom (Survival) checks.
Power You can add your Intelligence modifier to the damage of the first melee weapon attack you hit with on each of your turns.
Precision You can use a bonus action to focus your senses on a target you can see. Until the end of your turn, your weapon attacks against them score a critical hit on a roll of 19 or 20.
Speed Your walking speed increases by 10 feet, and you ignore nonmagical difficult terrain.
You can use a number of Mutagens equal to your Intelligence modifier (minimum of 1), and regain all uses when you finish a long rest. If you use a Mutagen while one is already in effect, the effect of your current Mutagen immediately ends
Alchemical Knight
By 6th level, your alchemical and martial skills improve. You can alchemically grant yourself a wider variety of traits. You ignore any Primary Mutation prerequisite when choosing the Secondary Mutation you gain this level and for all subsequent Secondary Mutations. You must still meet any other prerequisites, such as level or other Secondary Mutations. You also learn 2 maneuvers from the Battle Master list. If a maneuver you use requires your target to make a saving throw to resist the maneuver's effects, the saving throw DC equals 8 + your proficiency bonus + your Intelligence modifier. You gain 3 superiority dice, d6s, which can be used to fuel your maneuvers. A superiority die is expended when you use it. You regain your expended superiority dice when you roll initiative or finish a short or long rest.
Alchemical Knowledge
At 10th level, your alchemical knowledge improves. You can add double your proficiency bonus to any ability check you make with your Craft (Alchemy) Skill, and you gain proficiency in one of the following skills of your choice: Arcana, Investigation, History, Nature, or Religion. Your experience with alchemical reagents grants you advantage on saving throws against poison and disease, while your experience in combat allows you to learn another maneuver from the Battle Master list and increases your superiority dice to d8s.
Superior Mutagens
By 14th level, your mutagens are more potent. When you use a mutagen, its properties are enhanced as listed below:
Endurance While in combat, you gain temporary hit points equal to your Intelligence modifier + your proficiency bonus at the start of each of your turns. Any remaining temporary hit points vanish when combat ends.
Enhanced Reflexes When you are subjected to an effect that allows you to make a Dexterity saving throw to take only half damage, you instead take no damage if you succeed on the saving throw, and only half damage if you fail.
Enhanced Senses You have advantage on Wisdom (Perception) and Wisdom (Survival) checks.
Power You can add your Intelligence modifier to the damage of all melee weapon attacks you make.
Precision When you use your bonus action to focus your senses, your weapon attacks against the chosen target score a critical hit on a roll of 18, 19, or 20 until the end of your turn.
Speed Your walking speed increases by an additional 10 feet, and you can Dash as a bonus action on each of your turns.
Compound Mutagen
At 18th level, your mutagens are more stable and better able to synergize. If you consume a Mutagen while you already have one in effect, you can choose to gain the effects of both for one minute. After the minute passes, the effects of both Mutagens end, and you must make a DC 20 Constitution saving throw or suffer one level of exhaustion. Once you have used this ability, you cannot do so again until you finish a short or long rest.

Aquatic
Aquatic Nature
At 1st level, you grow gills and fins or webbed fingers and toes. These grant you a swim speed equal to your walking speed and allow you to breathe underwater.
Aquatic Feature
Also at 1st level, you have gained a feature common to many aquatic creatures. You gain one of the features of your choice listed below.
Boneless Limbs Your limbs are not supported by bones, but by semi-flexible cartilage and muscle, increasing your reach with melee weapons by 5 feet. Additionally, you have advantage on ability checks to escape a grapple or break free from non-magical restraints.
Grasping Pincer One of your arms has grown into a massive pincer or tentacle (your choice) capable of ripping armor or crushing enemies. This arm cannot hold weapons or a shield, but can be used as a natural weapon that you are proficient with and deals 1d8 slashing damage for a pincer or 1d8 bludgeoning damage for a tentacle on a hit. Once on each turn when you hit a Large or smaller creature with an attack with this limb, you can choose to Grapple it. While you have a creature grappled with this limb, you cannot attack another creature with it.
Siren Song Your vocal chords have mutated, allowing you to make strange and mesmerizing sounds. As an action, you can begin singing a haunting song, forcing creatures of your choice that can hear you within 30 feet of you to make a Wisdom saving throw (DC = 8 + your Charisma modifier + your proficiency bonus) or be charmed by you for one minute, until they can no longer hear you, or until they take any damage. A creature that succeeds on its saving throw is immune to this ability for 24 hours. You can use this ability a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus, and regain all uses when you finish a long rest.
Improved Aquatic Feature
By 6th level, the feature granted by your aquatic feature has grown more prominent and powerful, as listed below. In addition, you can choose one of the features listed under Aquatic Feature that you did not gain at level one as the Secondary Mutation that you gain this level.
Boneless Frame Your boneless form now allows you to escape a grapple as a bonus action and from nonmagical restraints as an action. Additionally, you have advantage on saving throws against effects that would paralyze you and opportunity attacks against you have disadvantage on the attack roll.
Crushing Pincer Your pincer or tentacle now deals 1d10 slashing or bludgeoning damage, and you can use a bonus action to slam a creature you have grappled with it into the ground, a wall, or another creature within 5 feet of you. The grappled creature takes bludgeoning damage equal to your Constitution modifier (minimum 1). If you slam them into another creature, it takes the same damage.
Haunting Song You can now begin your Siren Song as a bonus action, and when you sing your Siren Song, you can choose for creatures that fail their saving throws to become frightened of you, instead of charmed. When you use your Siren Song in this way, the frightened condition does not end for a target when it takes damage. Instead, they can repeat the save at the end of each of their turns, ending the effect on a success.
Wave Rider
At 10th level, you are even better adapted to life at sea. You gain resistance to cold damage, your swim speed increases by 30 feet, and you always know the direction to the nearest land mass while you are underwater or at sea.
Superior Aquatic Feature
By 14th level, the Aquatic Feature you gained at level 1 improves further, as listed below, and you can choose one of the features listed under Aquatic Feature that you do not possess as the Secondary Mutation that you gain this level.
Boneless Form Your boneless nature now allows you to squeeze through a space as narrow as 6 inches wide, provided you are carrying nothing and unarmored, and when you are hit by an attack, you can use your reaction to halve that attack's damage against you.
Deadly Pincer Your pincer or tentacle now deals 1d12 slashing or bludgeoning damage, you have advantage on attack rolls against creatures that you have grappled with your mutated limb, and such creatures have disadvantage on attack rolls against creatures other than you. In addition, you can now grapple Huge or smaller creatures with that limb.
Mesmerizing Song When you use your Siren Song to charm, creatures are incapacitated and have a movement speed of 0 for as long as they are charmed. When you use it to frighten, creatures have a movement speed of 0 for as long as they are frightened.
Deep Dweller
At 18th level, you are perfectly adapted to the massive pressure and icy chill of the depths. You gain immunity to cold damage, and whenever you take bludgeoning damage, you can reduce that damage by an amount equal to your Constitution modifier. This does not stack with the Heavy Armor Master feat or any other ability that similarly reduces bludgeoning damage.

Bestial
Bestial Nature
At 1st level, you gain proficiency in Survival and have advantage on Perception checks that rely on hearing or smell. If you are already proficient in Survival, you gain proficiency in another skill of your choice from the Mutant list.
Beastly Feature
At 1st level, you have gained an adaptation suited to the wilderness. You gain one of the features of your choice listed below:
Natural Weapons You grow claws, horns, or fangs (your choice) which you can use as natural melee weapons. You are proficient with your natural weapons, which deal 1d6 slashing damage if you chose claws, 1d6 bludgeoning damage if you chose horns, and 1d6 piercing damage if you chose fangs. When you take the Attack action and make an attack with your natural weapons, you can make one additional attack with them as a bonus action.
Powerful Legs Your legs are mutated to resemble a beast's, increasing your walking speed by 5 feet. If you move at least 20 feet toward a creature before hitting it with a melee weapon attack, you can roll the weapon's damage die one additional time and add the result to the damage roll.
Tough Hide You have grown a thick layer of protective fur, granting you an AC of 10 + your Dexterity modifier + your Constitution modifier while you are unarmored. You can wield a shield and still gain this benefit.
Improved Beastly Feature
At 6th level, the Beastly Feature you chose at level one is improved, as listed below. In addition, you can choose one of the features listed under Beastly Feature that you did not gain at level one as the Secondary Mutation that you gain this level.
Bleeding Wounds Your natural weapons now deal 1d8 damage of their type on a hit, and if you hit a creature with two or more attacks with your natural weapons on the same turn, it takes 1d8 necrotic damage at the end of its next turn as it bleeds from the wounds. Undead and Constructs are not affected by this ability.
Powerful Charger Your walking speed increases by an additional 5 feet and nonmagical difficult terrain no longer impedes your movement. If you move at least 20 feet toward a creature before hitting it with a melee weapon attack, you can roll the weapon's damage die two additional times and add the result to the damage roll.
Resilient Hide Your hide has adapted to grant you resistance to two of the following damage types of your choice: acid, cold, fire, or lightning.
Hunter's Instinct
By 10th level, your keen senses make you a superior hunter. You can add double your proficiency bonus to Survival checks and you have advantage on initiative rolls.
Superior Beastly Feature
At 14th level, the Beastly Feature that you gained at level 1 gains the improvement listed below, and you can choose one of the features listed under Beastly Feature that you do not possess as the Secondary Mutation that you gain this level.
Superior Natural Weapons The damage die for your natural weapons increases to 1d10, and when you cause a creature to bleed with them, they now take 2d8 necrotic damage at the end of their next turn.
Mauling Charge Your walking speed increases by an additional 5 feet, and when you deal damage to a creature with your Powerful Charger ability, you can immediately use a bonus action to deal additional bludgeoning damage to them equal to your Constitution modifier (minimum 1) and knock the target prone if it is Large or smaller.
Adaptive Hide When you take damage of any type except for psychic or poison, you can use your reaction to force your hide to adapt to that damage type, granting you resistance to the triggering damage type after you take the triggering damage. The resistance lasts until the next time you use this ability. Once you have used this ability, you cannot do so again until you finish a short or long rest.
Savagery
By 18th level, you have a savage nature to match your appearance. When you take the Attack action and all attacks made as part of that action hit the same target, the final attack deals an additional 2d6 damage of the weapon's type. When you take the Attack action and all attacks made as part of that action miss the same target, you can make an additional attack against that target as part of that action. You can only use one of the abilities offered by this feature once each turn, even if you have a feature like Action Surge that allows you to take the Attack action multiple times in one turn.

Monstrous
Monstrous Nature
At 1st level, you count as one size larger when determining your carrying capacity and the weight you can push, drag, or lift if your race does not already grant you that ability (as in the Goliath race). In addition, the first time on each of your turns that you hit a creature with a weapon attack that relies on Strength, you can add your Constitution modifier to the damage roll. When you reach level 11 in this class, you can add your Constitution modifier to the damage of all your weapon attacks that rely on Strength.
Natural Armor
Also at 1st level, you have grown a layer of chitin, fat, fur, scales, or other natural armor. While not wearing armor, your armor class is 12 + your Constitution modifier. For every 5 Mutant levels you attain, this increases by 1: to 13 + your Con modifier at level 6, 14 + your Con modifier at level 11, and 15 + your Con modifier at level 16. You can wield a shield and still gain this benefit.
Monstrous Feature
By 6th level, you have gained a prominent monstrous feature. You gain one of the features of your choice listed below. If a feature requires a saving throw, the DC is 8 + your Constitution modifier + your proficiency bonus:
Breath Weapon Like a mighty Dragon, you can spew forth a deadly elemental blast. Once on each of your turns, you can forgo one of your weapon attacks when you take the Attack action to breathe a 30 foot cone of acid, cold, fire, lightning, or poison (your choice when you gain this ability), dealing a number of d8s of damage equal to your proficiency bonus to creatures in the cone on a failed Dexterity saving throw, or half as much on a success. You can do this a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus. You regain one expended use when you finish a short rest and all uses when you finish a long rest.
Bulbous Eyes Your eyes have grown into hideous, unblinking orbs. As a bonus action, you can stare into the eyes of a creature within 60 feet of you that you can see and that can see you, forcing them to make a Wisdom saving throw. On a failure, the creature is frightened of you until the end of your next turn. Creatures that fail their saving throws against your stare by 5 or more are paralyzed for as long as they are frightened. On a success, a creature is immune to this ability for 24 hours.
Chimeric Aspect Like a Chimera, you've grown multiple bestial features. Your movement speed increases by 5 feet and you grow horns, claws, and fangs as natural weapons that you are proficient with. Your horns deal 1d8 bludgeoning damage, your claws deal 1d10 slashing damage, and your fangs deal 1d8 piercing damage. Once on each of your turns when you take the Attack action and hit the same creature with two different natural weapons (your horns and claws, for example), you can make an additional attack against the same target with your third natural weapon as part of that action.
Hulking Muscles You have grown the bulging, knotted muscles of an ogre. Once on each of your turns when you hit a creature that is no more than one size larger than you with a melee weapon attack, you can push it up to 10 feet away from you, and melee weapons you wield have their damage die increased by one step. From 1d4 to 1d6, 1d6 to 1d8, 1d8 to 1d10, 1d10 to 1d12, or from 1d12 or 2d6 to 2d8. In addition, if you have at least one hand free, you can use an action to throw a boulder or other suitable piece of terrain within 5 feet of you that is your size or one size smaller. This counts as a ranged weapon attack with which you are proficient with a normal range of 20 feet and a long range of 60 feet. It deals a number of d10s of bludgeoning damage equal to your proficiency bonus on a hit, and uses your Strength modifier for the attack and damage roll.
More Monster Than Man
By 10th level, you are recognized as a monstrous creature. You gain proficiency in Intimidation. If you are already proficient, you can add double your proficiency bonus to Charisma (Intimidation checks). In addition, choose one of the following creature types: Aberrations, Dragons, Giants, or Monstrosities. You learn one language associated with such creatures and have advantage on Charisma checks made when interacting with them. At your DM's discretion, you can instead learn a different language of your choice.
Superior Monstrous Feature
By 14th level, your monstrous features have become even more prominent. The Monstrous Feature that you chose at level six gains the improvement listed below. If a feature requires a saving throw, the DC is 8 + your Constitution modifier + your proficiency bonus:
Powerful Breath Your breath weapon has grown considerably more potent. You can now choose to breathe a 30 or 60 foot cone each time you use your breath weapon, and your breath weapon's damage dice increase to d10s when you choose a 60 foot cone or d12s when you choose a 30 foot cone.
Manifold Eyes You have grown additional bulbous eyeballs in various places around your body. This grants you advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on sight and immunity to the blinded condition. In addition, frightened creatures have disadvantage on their saving throws against your Bulbous Eyes, and when you use your Bulbous Eyes to frighten a creature, you can instead target each creature of your choice that you can see and can see you within 30 feet of you.
Chimeric Rage Your chimeric features have grown more prominent. Your movement speed increases by an additional 5 feet, the damage dice for your horns and fangs increase to 1d10, and the damage die for your claws increases to 1d12. When you hit with the additional attack gained from your Chimeric Aspect ability, it deals 1d10 bludgeoning, 1d10 piercing, and 1d12 slashing damage on a hit, and you can immediately grapple the target, knock it prone, or push it 5 feet away from you if it is no more than one size larger than you.
Massive Muscles Your twisted muscles have grown even more massive. You can wield melee weapons with the 2- handed property in a single hand, though you cannot engage in two-weapon fighting with weapons with the heavy property. Weapons with the 2-handed property that you wield with both hands have their damage die increased by an additional step: from 1d10 to 1d12, 1d12 or 2d6 to 2d8, or from 2d8 to 2d10. You can also forgo one of your attacks when you take the Attack action to throw a creature you are grappling that is your size or smaller up to 15 feet away from you. It must make a Dexterity saving throw, taking 4d10 bludgeoning damage and falling prone on a failure. On a success, it takes half the damage and isn't knocked prone. If you throw a creature to a space occupied by another creature, it must make a Dexterity saving throw or suffer the same effects, and the creature you threw lands in the nearest unoccupied space.
Truly Monstrous
At 18th level, your features have twisted to the point that you can no longer be recognized as truly humanoid. Your creature type changes to Aberration, Dragon, Giant, or Monstrosity (your choice) and your size increases from Small to Medium or from Medium to Large. You have advantage on Strength checks and saving throws, and your weapon attacks deal an additional 1d4 damage on a hit.

Venomous
Venomous Feature
At 1st level, you've developed a gland that produces a powerful venom. You gain one of the features of your choice listed below. If a feature requires a saving throw, the DC is 8 + your Constitution modifier + your proficiency bonus:
Fangs You grow claws or fangs which can be used as natural melee weapons to deliver your venom. You are proficient with your natural weapons, which deal 1d4 piercing damage (for fangs) or 1d4 slashing damage (for claws). You can use your Dexterity modifier for attack and damage rolls with them. Once on each of your turns when you hit a creature with your natural weapons, you can force the target to make a Constitution saving throw. They take a number of d6s of poison damage equal to your proficiency bonus on a failed save, or half as much on a success. You can do this a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus, and regain all uses when you finish a long rest.
Secretions Your skin continually secretes small amounts of venom. At the start of each of your turns, you can apply your secretions to a slashing or piercing melee weapon, or a piece of ammunition (no action required). The first time you hit a creature with that weapon or ammunition before the end of your turn, the target takes an additional 1d6 poison damage.
Spit When you take the Attack action, you can forgo one of your attacks to spit a stream of poison at a creature you can see within 30 feet of you, forcing them to make a Dexterity saving throw. On a failure, they are blinded until the end of your next turn. You can do this a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus, and regain all uses when you finish a long rest.
Venomous Nature
Also at 1st level, you gain proficiency with the Craft (Poison) skill, and you have advantage on saving throws against poison.
Improved Venomous Feature
At 6th level, the Venemous Feature you chose at level one is improved as listed below. You can also choose one of the features listed under Venomous Feature that you did not gain at level 1 as the Secondary Mutation that you gain this level.
Dazing Venom The damage of the your Fangs' venom increases to a number of d8s equal to half your Mutant level (round up). When a creature fails its saving throw against your Fangs' venom, it is poisoned for 1 minute. It can repeat the save at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on a success. If you roll initiative and have no uses of your Fangs remaining, you regain one use.
Contact Venom The damage dealt by your Secretions increases to 1d6 + half your Mutant level, and when you are grappled, hit by an unarmed strike, or hit by a natural melee weapon (such as a wolf's bite attack), you can use your reaction to deal your Secretion's damage to the aggressor. You can also deal this damage once on each of your turns when you grapple a creature.
Slowing Spit In addition to being blinded, a creature that fails its saving throw against your Poison Spit can take an action, a bonus action, or a move action on each of its turns for one minute unless it is immune to being poisoned. It can make a Constitution saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending this additional effect on a success. The blindness effect still ends at the end of your next turn. If you roll initiative and have no uses of your Spit remaining, you regain one use.
Potent Venom
By 10th level, your venomous nature grants you resistance to poison damage, and the poison damage you deal with your Mutant features ignores resistance to poison damage.
Superior Venomous Feature
At 14th level, the Venomous Feature that you chose at level 1 gains the improvement listed below. You can also choose one of the features listed under Venomous Feature that you don't possess as the Secondary Mutation that you gain this level.
Stunning Venom A creature that fails its saving throw against the venom delivered by your Fangs (or claws) is stunned for as long as it is poisoned. The first time it succeeds on a saving throw at the end of its turn, it ends the stunned condition, but not the poisoned condition. The second time it succeeds, the poisoned condition ends.
Greater Secretions When you use your Secretions to coat a melee weapon, you deal your Secretion damage the first two times you hit with that weapon before the end of your turn. When you use your Secretions to coat ammunition for a ranged weapon, you can coat two pieces of ammunition at once.
Spraying Spit When you use your Spit, you can spit a line of venom that is 30 feet long and 5 feet wide. Each creature in the line must make a Dexterity saving throw or suffer the effects of your Spit.
Deadly Venom
By 18th level, your venom is uniquely deadly. You gain immunity to poison damage and the poisoned condition, and once on each of your turns when you deal poison damage with a Mutant feature, you can add your Constitution modifier to the damage dealt. In addition, when you deal poison damage to a creature that is normally immune to poison with a Mutant feature, it instead takes half the normal amount of damage.

Secondary Mutations
Adapted Immunity
Prerequisite: 18th level
Your Adapted Resistance feature now grants you immunity to the condition you chose. Camouflage Your hide, skin, or scales can shift colors to match your surroundings. If you spend at least 10 minutes in terrain of a certain type, such as forest, urban, or desert, you have advantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks while not wearing armor until you leave that terrain.
Corrosive Venom
Prerequisite: 10th level, Venomous Primary Mutation
You've mutated to produce especially caustic venom. Whenever you would normally deal poison damage with a Mutant feature, you can choose to deal acid damage instead.
Deadly Natural Weapons
Prerequisite: 3rd level, A natural weapon granted by your Primary Mutation or the Spines mutation
Your natural weapons count as magic for the purpose of overcoming damage resistances.
Dweller in Trees and Mountains
Prerequisite: Bestial or Venomous Primary Mutation
You have gained a feature useful to life in the mountains or the treetops. You gain a climb speed equal to your walking speed, and you can climb while keeping one arm free. When you make a running jump, the distance you cover increases by a number of feet equal to your Constitution modifier.
Echolocation
Prerequisite: 18th level, Bestial or Aquatic Primary Mutation
You have developed the ability to use the echolocation of a bat or sonar of an aquatic mammal. While you are not deafened, you have blindsight to a range of 30 feet.
Feral Speech
Your mutation has made you more at home among animals than people. You can communicate simple ideas with beasts through sounds and gestures and understand their intentions by interpreting their body language and grunts, growls, chirps, or other sounds.
Greater Spines
Prerequisite: 14th level, Spines Mutation
Your spines grow longer, sharper, and more dangerous. Whenever you are hit with an unarmed strike or a melee attack with a natural weapon, such as a wolf's bite, the attacker takes 1d6 piercing damage. If you have the Deadly Natural Weapons mutation, this damage counts as magical for the purpose of overcoming damage resistances.
Healing Surge
Prerequisite: 3rd level
Your mutation allows you to rapidly recover from injuries. You gain the following Reserves of Strength option: Healing Surge As a bonus action, you can expend a hit die and roll it, regaining hit points equal to the result.
Hibernation
Prerequisite: 14th level,
You do not have the Nocturnal Mutation You've adapted to gain more restful sleep. When you finish a long rest, you regain an additional number of expended hit dice equal to your Constitution modifier (minimum 1).
Hunter's Howl
Prerequisite: 10th level, Bestial Primary Mutation
With a roar or howl, you can rally your pack to the hunt. As a bonus action, you can howl to inspire yourself and a number of willing creatures that can hear you up to your Constitution modifier (minimum 1). Each gains temporary hit points equal to your Constitution modifier (minimum 1) and has advantage on the first attack roll they make before the end of your next turn. Once you have used this ability, you cannot do so again until you finish a short or long rest.
Improved Healing
With enough time, your wounds heal faster than most. Whenever you roll a hit die to recover hit points at the end of a short rest and roll a 1 or 2 on the die, you can reroll that hit die. You must use the new result, even if it is a 1 or a 2.
Ink Spray
Prerequisite: 10th level, Aquatic Primary Mutation
Your mutation allows you to spray ink for quick escapes. As a bonus action, you can spray ink, forcing each creature within 10 feet of you to make a Dexterity saving throw. On a failure, a creature is blinded and cannot take reactions until the end of the current turn. Once you have used this ability, you cannot do so again until you finish a short or long rest.
Limit Removal
Prerequisite: 6th level
Your mutation allows you to surpass ordinary limits at great cost. If you have no hit dice remaining, you can still use your Reserves of Strength abilities. When you do, roll a d12 and use the ability as normal. You then take necrotic damage equal to the roll's result, ignoring resistances and immunities. On a roll of 11 or 12, you gain a level of exhaustion instead of taking necrotic damage.
Martial Adaptation
Your body has become naturally suited toward wielding more powerful weaponry. You gain proficiency with martial weapons.


Monstrous Presence
Prerequisite: 10th level, Monstrous Primary Mutation
Your presence is enough to cause enemies to focus their attention on you. As an action, you can roar or present yourself as the largest threat on the field. Creatures of your choice within 30 feet of you that can see or hear you must make a Wisdom saving throw. A creature that is immune to being frightened automatically succeeds. On a failure, a creature has disadvantage on attack rolls against creatures other than you for 1 minute. A creature can repeat the save at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on a success. On a success, a creature is immune to this ability for 1 hour.
Nocturnal
Prerequisite: You do not have the Hibernation Mutation
You have better adapted to the night and rarely need to sleep. You gain darkvision to a range of 120 feet and can gain the benefits of a long rest by spending 1 hour asleep and 7 hours performing light activity, such as keeping watch.
Physical Adaptation
You have gained features that help you perform physical activities. Choose two skills that rely on Strength or Dexterity. You gain proficiency in those skills.
Poison Gas
Prerequisite: 10th level, Venomous Mutation
You've mutated to expel your venom as a gas. As an action, you can exhale a 15 foot cone of toxic gas. Creatures within the cone must make a Constitution saving throw. On a failure, a creature takes a number of d6s of poison damage equal to your proficiency bonus and is poisoned until the end of your next turn. While poisoned by this ability, a creature's movement speed is halved and they cannot take reactions. On a success they take half as much damage and aren't poisoned. Once you have used this ability, you cannot do so again until you finish a short or long rest.
Quadrupedal
Prerequisite: 6th level
Your mutated form can easily shift between standing upright and crawling or running on all fours. It takes you only 5 feet of movement to stand up from being prone, crawling does not cost you extra movement, being prone does not impose disadvantage on your melee weapon attack rolls unless you are using a two handed weapon, and when you are targeted by a melee attack while you are prone, you can use your reaction to negate the advantage on the attack roll. Until the end of the current turn, melee attacks do not have advantage against you due to your being prone.
Rampage
Prerequisite: 14th level, Savage Resilience Mutation
When you are reduced to 0 hit points but not killed outright, you can gain temporary hit points equal to twice your Mutant level and remain conscious. You still must make death saving throws, and you suffer the normal effects of taking damage while at 0 hit points. While conscious but at 0 hit points, you have advantage on melee weapon attack rolls and saving throws other than death saving throws. If you reach 3 failed death saving throws while in this state, you die. Once you have used this ability, you can't do so again until you finish a long rest.
Regeneration
Prerequisite: 18th level, Healing Surge Mutation
Your healing abilities have advanced further. Over the course of a long rest, you can regrow a missing body part, such as a severed limb or missing eye. When you use this ability, you do not recover hit dice during the long rest and your levels of exhaustion are not reduced.
Savage Resilience
Prerequisite: Bestial or Monstrous Primary Mutation
When you are reduced to 0 hit points but not killed outright, you can drop to 1 hit point instead. You can’t use this feature again until you finish a long rest.
Scaly Skin
Prerequisite: Venomous or Aquatic Primary Mutation
You have grown a thin layer of scales or a protective carapace. While not wearing armor, your AC is 13 + your Dexterity modifier. You can use a shield and still gain this benefit.
Spines
You grow small but dangerous quills or spines. Creatures that are grappled by you or grappling you take 1d6 piercing damage at the start of each of their turns. If you have the Deadly Natural Weapons secondary mutation, this damage counts as magical for the purpose of overcoming damage resistances.
Superior Camouflage
Prerequisite: 14th level, Camouflage Mutation
Your ability to blend into your surroundings improves. While unarmored, you can use an action to force your skin, hide or scales to flawlessly match your surroundings, effectively becoming invisible for 1 hour or until you make an attack, cast a spell, or force a creature to make a saving throw. Once you have used this ability, you cannot do so again until you have finished a short or long rest.
Superior Vigor
Prerequisite: 10th level, Vigorous Health Mutation
Your mutation now keeps you in peak form. When you finish a short rest, you gain temporary hit points equal to half your Mutant level + your Constitution modifier. When you finish a long rest, you now gain temporary hit points equal to your Mutant level + your Constitution modifier.
Terrifying Visage
Your mutation has granted you features that are terrifying to behold, but useless in combat. You have advantage on Charisma (Intimidation) checks, but disadvantage on Charisma (Persuasion) checks when dealing with humanoids.
Vigorous Health
Your mutation keeps you in good health. You are immune to disease and gain temporary hit points equal to your Mutant level whenever you finish a long rest.
Voracity
You've developed a hunger fed only by battle. When you score a critical hit with a weapon attack, you gain temporary hit points equal to your Constitution modifier (minimum of 1 temporary hit point).
