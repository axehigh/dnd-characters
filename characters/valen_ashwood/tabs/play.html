How to Play Valen Ashwood

Valen is an alchemical missile artillery specialist who bombards the battlefield with deadly ranged munitions, alchemical bolts, and precision sniper volleys, while remaining exceptionally resilient out of combat and in the thick of a fight.

Out of Combat Options

Master Alchemist & Crafter

Equipped with proficiency in Craft (Alchemy) (+7) and Investigation/Insight, Valen can brew alchemical explosives, poisons, healing balms, and analyze unknown magical or chemical substances during investigations.

Survival & Exploration

With proficiencies in Athletics, Insight, Perception, and Survival, Valen can scout ahead, track targets across rough wilderness, and read the intentions of suspicious individuals.

Reserves of Strength (Steady Nerves)

If you ever fail an important skill check in which you are proficient, use a reaction to expend a hit die and add the roll to your check, turning failure into success!

In Combat Options (Missile Artillery)

Sniper & Sharpshooter Volleys

Equipped with Heavy Armor (AC 18) and the Archery Fighting Style (+2 to ranged attacks), Valen fires heavy crossbow bolts with supreme accuracy (+7 total attack bonus). Use Sharpshooter (-5 to attack, +10 to damage) combined with Archery style to pierce through heavy armor and obliterate priority targets.

Alchemical Maneuvers (Battle Master)

Infuse your ranged missile attacks with Battle Master maneuvers using your superiority dice (d6s). Use Pushing Attack to blast enemies backward off ledges or away from allies, and Distracting Strike to leave foes wide open for your party's next attacks.

Reserves of Strength (Steady Arm & Burst of Power)

Missed an attack? Use Steady Arm (Reaction + Hit Die) to turn the miss into a hit! Land a critical hit? Use Burst of Power to roll a hit die and add twice the result to your critical damage.
