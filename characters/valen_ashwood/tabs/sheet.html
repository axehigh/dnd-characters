Description & information

Ranged Attack Bonus: Proficiency (+4) + Dexterity (+3) = +7.

Alchemical Mutation DC: 8 + Constitution (+3) + Proficiency (+4) = 15.

Missile Artillery: Valen specializes in sending volleys of bolts infused with volatile alchemical concoctions from heavy ranged weaponry across the battlefield.

Features

  • Human Versatility Racial trait

    +1 to all ability scores, extra skill proficiency, and an extra feat.

  • Bowman of the Guild Cultural feat

    Specialized training in ranged weapons and missile volleys, increasing precision and missile range.

  • Fierce Shot Cultural feat

    Human cultural feat (level 5). Selected to sharpen aimed, high-impact ranged fire.

  • Natural Watchfulness Cultural feat

    Human cultural feat (level 9). Selected to support scouting, positioning, and battlefield awareness.

  • Primary Mutation: Alchemical Mutant 1

    Mutant class abilities and Secondary Mutations use Intelligence/Constitution for DCs. Proficiency with Craft (Alchemy) and martial weapons. Replaced secondary mutations with Heavy Armor proficiency and Archery Fighting Style.

  • Secondary Mutations & Heavy Armor Mutant 2

    Gained heavy armor proficiency and Archery Fighting Style in place of secondary mutations (+2 to ranged weapon attack rolls).

  • Reserves of Strength Mutant 3

    Expend hit dice for Steady Arm (turn a missed attack into a hit) or Steady Nerves (turn a failed ability check into a success).

  • Extra Attack Mutant 5

    You can attack twice, instead of once, whenever you take the Attack action on your turn.

  • Alchemical & Martial Mastery Mutant 6

    Learned 2 maneuvers from the Battle Master list (Pushing Attack, Distracting Strike). Gain 3 superiority dice (d6s) that refresh on initiative, short, or long rest. Save DC = 8 + proficiency (+4) + Intelligence (+3) = 15.

  • Improved Reserves of Strength Mutant 7

    Gain Burst of Power: when you score a critical hit with a weapon attack, expend a hit die and roll it, adding twice the result to the critical damage.

  • Adapted Resistance Mutant 9

    Adapted resilience and defenses against harsh conditions and battlefield hazards.

  • Sharpshooter General feat

    Attacking at long range doesn't impose disadvantage on your ranged weapon attacks. Your ranged weapon attacks ignore half cover and three-quarters cover. Before you make an attack with a ranged weapon that you are proficient with, you can choose to take a -5 penalty to the attack roll. If the attack hits, you add +10 to the attack's damage.

  • Lucky General feat

    Human Versatility extra feat (level 1). You have 3 luck points; spend one to roll an additional d20 on an attack roll, ability check, or saving throw, or to force an attacker to reroll. Regain expended luck points on a long rest.

  • Crossbow Expert General feat

    Level 3. Ignore the loading property of crossbows you are proficient with; being within 5 feet of a hostile creature doesn't impose disadvantage on your ranged attacks; when you take the Attack action with a one-handed weapon, you can make one attack with a hand crossbow as a bonus action.

  • Alert General feat

    Level 7. +5 to initiative; you can't be surprised while conscious; other creatures don't gain advantage on attack rolls against you from being hidden.
