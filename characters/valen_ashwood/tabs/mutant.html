Mutant Rules & Alchemical Progression

  • Hit Dice & Saves: 1d12 per Mutant level. Saving Throw proficiencies in Constitution and Charisma.
  • Primary Mutation (Alchemical): Uses Intelligence to determine saving throw DCs, temporary hit points, and alchemical properties. Grants Craft (Alchemy) proficiency and martial weapon proficiency.
  • Customized Mutations (Campaign Rules): Replaced secondary mutations at level 2 with Heavy Armor Proficiency and the Archery Fighting Style (+2 to ranged weapon attacks).
  • Reserves of Strength (Level 3): Expend hit dice for Steady Arm (turn a miss into a hit) or Steady Nerves (turn a failed skill check into a success).
  • Extra Attack (Level 5): Attack twice whenever you take the Attack action.
  • Alchemical & Martial Mastery (Level 6): Learned 2 Battle Master maneuvers (Pushing Attack, Distracting Strike) with 3 superiority dice (d6s) that refresh on initiative, short, or long rest. Save DC = 15.
  • Improved Reserves of Strength (Level 7): Gain Burst of Power (add twice an expended hit die's roll to critical hit damage).
  • Adapted Resistance (Level 9): Chosen condition: Frightened. Advantage on saving throws against effects that inflict Frightened and on saves to end it.
