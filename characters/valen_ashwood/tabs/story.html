Character Story & Description

Valen Ashwood

Overview

Valen Ashwood is a human mutant and alchemical artillery specialist: a calm, calculating marksman who turns volatile reagents into long-range devastation. He is not a frontline brawler; he is the one who holds the ridge, counts the wind, and makes the shot that ends the fight before it reaches the line. His mutations are the cost of the research that made him dangerous, written across his body in glowing veins and alchemical scarring.

Backstory (draft)

Valen began as a researcher rather than a soldier. Trained in alchemy and siegecraft, he studied how volatile compounds could be weaponized without the fragility of spellcasting — how a bolt, properly charged, could carry fire, shrapnel, or force farther and more reliably than any cantrip. The work was theoretical until a border conflict put his garrison under siege. With the walls failing and the powder stores thin, Valen volunteered to test his experimental mutagens on himself, betting that a single alchemist with a heavy crossbow and the right reagents could do the work of a battery of artillery.

The gamble worked, and it cost him. The mutagens took root: his veins now glow faintly green where the alchemical agents pooled, his reflexes sharpened beyond human limits, and his body adapted to the recoil and the fumes. He survived the siege, but the mutations did not fade. Rather than hide them, he refined them — learning to control the changes the way he controlled a reaction in a crucible, turning instability into precision.

Since then, Valen has worked as a mercenary and specialist for hire: the one a company brings in when a monster or a fortification needs to be brought down from a distance. He is methodical about it — scouting positions, reading terrain, preparing the right bolts for the right targets, and waiting for the moment when a single shot (or a volley) turns the field. He does not chase glory; he solves problems at range and lets the melee fighters finish what his bolts start.

The Adapted Resistance he chose is Frightened: a sniper who has stared down charging lines and charging monsters alike has learned to master fear rather than be mastered by it. Whether that calm is natural or a side effect of the mutagens, he does not say.

Personality & Mannerisms

Methodical & Patient

Valen does not rush. He reads the field, prepares the right munition, and waits for the shot that matters. Long explanations suit him; he thinks in variables and contingencies.

Quietly Confident

Low drama, high competence. He speaks plainly about what he can do and does not oversell it. His confidence is in the work, not the posture.

Loyal to the Contract

He treats an agreement the way he treats a formula: precisely and completely. Once committed to a party or a job, he sees it through, even when the odds turn.

Unflappable Under Fire

Adapted Resistance (Frightened) and years of holding a firing line have made him difficult to rattle. He is the one who keeps shooting when others panic.

Appearance

Heavy alchemical mail over a hardened breastplate, with spiked pauldrons and reinforced gauntlets; a round shield is carried but usually stowed in favor of a two-handed crossbow grip. Alchemical vials and bolt quivers at the belt, a portable lab kit on the back. Faintly glowing green veins trace his forearms and temples where the mutagens pooled; his eyes catch light with a faint alchemical sheen. The look is less "soldier" than "walking laboratory that happens to be armed."

Combat Style

Valen fights at range. He holds a fortified position in heavy armor (AC 18), fires the alchemical heavy crossbow at +7 (+9 with Archery style, or +4 with Sharpshooter's -5 for +10 damage), and uses Crossbow Expert to ignore loading and fire in melee. Pushing Attack blasts enemies off ledges or away from allies; Distracting Strike sets up melee allies. Reserves of Strength (Steady Arm, Steady Nerves, Burst of Power) keeps crucial shots from missing and turns criticals into bursts. Mutagens (3/long rest) tune him to the threat. He is an AoE controller as much as a sniper: shrapnel bolts, pushed enemies, and forced repositioning shape the battlefield while the front line holds.

Recorded decisions

Adapted Resistance (level 9): Frightened — advantage on saves against effects that inflict Frightened and on saves to end it.
