Human Rules & Campaign Feats

  • Human Versatility: +1 to all six ability scores, proficiency in an extra skill (Insight), and an extra feat.
  • Cultural Feat (Bowman of the Guild): Specialized training in ranged missile volleys, increasing precision and range with bows and crossbows.
  • General Feat (Sharpshooter): Ignore long range penalties and half/three-quarters cover. Take a -5 penalty to attack rolls to add +10 to ranged weapon damage.
