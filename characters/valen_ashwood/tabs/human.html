Human Rules & Campaign Feats

  • Human Versatility: +1 to all six ability scores, proficiency in an extra skill (Insight), and an extra feat. Recorded ability scores: STR 14, DEX 16, CON 16, INT 16, WIS 12, CHA 12.
  • Level 1 · Cultural · Herbal Remedies: Rural human cultural feat. Poison remedies: neutralise a poison as an action with a DC 10 Medicine check by expending a Hit Die; create proficiency-bonus concoctions during a long rest granting advantage on poison saves for 1 hour. Can be selected twice.
  • Level 1 · General · Sharpshooter: Ignore long range penalties and half/three-quarters cover. Take a -5 penalty to attack rolls to add +10 to ranged weapon damage.
  • Level 1 · General (Human Versatility extra feat) · Mobile: Your speed increases by 10 feet; when you make a melee attack against a creature, you don't provoke opportunity attacks from that creature for the rest of the turn; when you take the Dash action, difficult terrain doesn't cost extra movement.
  • Level 3 · General · Crossbow Expert: Ignore the loading property of crossbows you are proficient with; being within 5 feet of a hostile creature doesn't impose disadvantage on your ranged attacks; when you take the Attack action with a one-handed weapon, you can make one attack with a hand crossbow as a bonus action.
  • Level 5 · Cultural · A Hunter's Resolve: Rural human cultural feat. As a reaction, expend a Hit Die for immediate hit point recovery in combat; cannot be used again until a short or long rest.
  • Level 7 · General · Alert: +5 to initiative; you can't be surprised while conscious; other creatures don't gain advantage on attack rolls against you from being hidden.
  • Level 9 · Cultural · Natural Watchfulness: Human cultural feat (campaign list) selected to support scouting, positioning, and battlefield awareness.

All feat slots through level 9 are now recorded. Human subtype: Rural. Cultural feats are taken from the Rural list. Adapted Resistance (level 9) is locked in as Frightened.
