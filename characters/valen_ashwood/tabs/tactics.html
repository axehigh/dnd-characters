◇ Combat Tactics

1. The Alchemical Sharpshooter Missile Routine

  • Positioning: Stand back in heavy armor at range, utilizing the Heavy Crossbow.
  • Attack Bonus Calculation: Proficiency (+4) + Dexterity (+3) + Archery Fighting Style (+2) = +9 to hit (or +4 when using Sharpshooter's -5 penalty).
  • Sharpshooter Volley: When firing at high-value targets or monsters with low AC, toggle Sharpshooter for +10 flat damage per hit.

2. Superiority Maneuvers at Range

  • Pushing Attack: When an enemy closes in or tries to rush your backline, hit them with a heavy crossbow bolt infused with explosive force, forcing a Strength save (DC 15) or pushing them 15 feet away.
  • Distracting Strike: Hit an enemy to distract them, granting your next melee ally advantage on their attack against that target.

3. Reserves of Strength Engine

  • Steady Arm: Never let a crucial missile attack miss. If you miss by 1 to 12 points, expend a hit die as a reaction and add it to the roll to turn it into a hit.
  • Burst of Power: When scoring a critical hit, expend a hit die and roll it, adding twice the result to your critical damage for massive alchemical burst output.
