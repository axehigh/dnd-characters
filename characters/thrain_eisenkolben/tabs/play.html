How to play Thrain Eisenkolben

Recorded concept: a dwarf Witcher / alchemist, using the Mutant class with Alchemical Primary Mutation and Alchemical Knight. This guide explains the build, not an invented personality or backstory.

Before a fight

Choose one mutagen to match the threat. Precision supports critical hits against a focused target; Endurance grants 4 temporary HP at the start of each combat turn. You cannot have both active. Each consumption uses a bonus action and one of four uses per long rest.

Precision's target focus also needs a bonus action and lasts until end of turn. Activate ahead of combat when possible; do not spend two bonus actions in one turn.

On initiative, refresh your three d6 superiority dice. Confirm equipment and AC before resolving attacks; they are not yet supplied.

Healing is a resource decision

Dodge with Dwarf Fortitude to spend a Hit Die and heal 1d12+2. Healing Surge instead uses a bonus action and a Hit Die to heal its roll, without an automatic ability modifier. Durable sets minimum HP regained to 4.

Both draw from the same 9d12 Hit Dice pool used for Reserves of Strength. Keep track of spent dice. Limit Removal only works once out of Hit Dice and costs necrotic damage equal to the replacement d12 roll.

Control and investigation

Trip Attack and Disarming Attack are the two known maneuvers; use them for control with DC 16 and d6 superiority dice. Use Craft (Alchemy) +8, Investigation +8, and Perception +4 outside combat. Enhanced Senses can improve Perception and Survival while active, replacing any previous mutagen.

Personal motives, voice, bonds, and background remain for the player to define. Low Charisma does not prescribe a personality.
