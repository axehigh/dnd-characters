How to play Thrain Eisenkolben

Recorded concept: a dwarf Witcher / alchemist, using the Mutant class with Alchemical Primary Mutation and Alchemical Knight. This guide explains the build, not an invented personality or backstory.

Before a fight

Choose one mutagen to match the threat. Precision supports critical hits against a focused target; Endurance grants 3 temporary HP at the start of each of your turns in combat. You cannot have both active. Each consumption uses a bonus action and one of three uses per long rest.

Precision's target focus also needs a bonus action and lasts until end of turn. Activate ahead of combat when possible; do not spend two bonus actions in one turn.

On initiative, refresh your three d6 superiority dice. The warhammer attacks at +6 to hit for 1d8+2, or 1d10+2 with two hands; Fell-handed raises that to 1d8+4 or 1d10+4. Chain mail and shield give AC 18, or AC 16 with the shield stowed. Stowing the shield frees a hand for alchemical substances and enables the two-handed warhammer grip.

Healing is a resource decision

Dodge with Dwarf Fortitude to spend a Hit Die and heal 1d12+3. Healing Surge instead uses a bonus action and a Hit Die to heal its roll, without an automatic ability modifier. Durable sets minimum HP regained to 6.

Both draw from the same 9d12 Hit Dice pool used for Reserves of Strength. Keep track of spent dice; with no Hit Dice left, these healing and Reserves options cannot be used.

Control and investigation

Selected secondary mutations: Spines and Nocturnal at level 2, Quadrupedal at level 6, Healing Surge at level 9. Spines damages creatures grappling you or grappled by you for 1d6 piercing at the start of each of their turns. Quadrupedal supports prone positioning and standing for 5 ft of movement.

Trip Attack and Disarming Attack are the two known maneuvers; use them for control with DC 15 and d6 superiority dice. Use Craft (Alchemy) +7, Investigation +7, and Perception +5 outside combat. Enhanced Senses adds +3 while active: Perception +8 and Survival +4 (normally +1, not proficient), replacing any previous mutagen.

Nocturnal grants darkvision to 120 ft, separate from dwarf low-light vision to 60 ft. A long rest still takes 8 hours: 1 hour asleep and 7 hours of light activity, such as keeping watch. This is not Hibernation and does not grant extra Hit Dice recovery.

After a creature damages you, Fury grants +2 to attack and damage against that creature until the end of your first round after taking the damage. Track the attacker and the expiry; this is not a passive bonus against every target.

Personal motives, voice, bonds, and background remain for the player to define. Low Charisma does not prescribe a personality.
