The campaign grants two secondary mutations at level 2 and one more at levels 6 and 9. All prerequisites apply: Spines has none; Nocturnal requires not having Hibernation; Quadrupedal requires level 6; Healing Surge requires level 3 and is selected at level 9. Hibernation is not selected. No additional unselected mutation, Fighting Style, or equipment is assumed.

Alchemical Nature & Warrior

All Mutant class and secondary mutation properties normally based on Constitution use Intelligence (+3) instead. Mutant save DC: 8 + PB 4 + INT 3 = 15.

Craft (Alchemy) and martial weapon proficiency. Recorded proficiencies: light, medium, heavy armor, shields, simple and martial weapons; Constitution and Charisma saves. Heavy armor comes from the recorded feat, not an extra secondary mutation.

Hit Dice: 9d12. Extra Attack at level 5: two attacks with the Attack action. Total HP is 124, recorded on the sheet. Equipped chain mail and shield give AC 18 (16 with the shield stowed), recorded on the Inventory tab.

Mutagens · 3 / long rest

Consume as a bonus action for a one-hour effect. Only one mutagen can be active; consuming another ends the current one.

  • Precision: while active, use a bonus action to focus on a visible target. Weapon attacks against that target crit on 19–20 until the end of your turn. Activation and focusing both use bonus actions; do not assume both in the same turn.
  • Endurance: gain 3 temporary HP at the start of each of your turns in combat. Remaining temporary HP vanish when combat ends; do not add successive grants together.
  • Power: add +3 damage to the first melee weapon attack you hit with on each of your turns.
  • Speed: +10 ft walking speed (40 ft total) and ignore nonmagical difficult terrain.
  • Enhanced Reflexes: +3 to Dexterity checks and saving throws (Dexterity saves +3, Acrobatics +7 while active).
  • Enhanced Senses: +3 to Perception and Survival checks (Perception +8, Survival +4 while active).

Alchemical Knight · level 6

3 superiority dice, d6s; expend a die to fuel a maneuver. Regain expended dice when rolling initiative or finishing a short or long rest. Maneuver save DC 15.

Two maneuvers known: Trip Attack and Disarming Attack. Menacing Attack is not recorded as known.

  • Trip Attack: on a weapon hit, spend a superiority die and add it to damage; a Large or smaller target makes a Strength save (DC 15) or falls prone.
  • Disarming Attack: on a weapon hit, spend a superiority die and add it to damage; target makes a Strength save (DC 15) or drops one chosen held item at its feet.

From level 6, ignore Primary Mutation prerequisites when selecting secondary mutations; all other prerequisites still apply.

Reserves of Strength

  • Steady Arm: reaction when a weapon attack misses; spend a Hit Die and add the roll to the attack, possibly turning it into a hit.
  • Power Through: reaction when hit; spend a Hit Die and subtract the roll from damage before resistances or vulnerabilities.
  • Burst of Power: on a weapon critical hit, spend a Hit Die and add double its roll to damage. No reaction is specified.

All share the 9d12 Hit Dice pool with healing. Expended dice cannot also be spent on short-rest healing; track them until recovered through rest.

Secondary mutations

  • Spines · level 2: creatures grappling you or grappled by you take 1d6 piercing at the start of each of their turns. No magical damage upgrade is recorded.
  • Nocturnal · level 2: darkvision to 120 ft; gain the benefits of a long rest with 1 hour asleep and 7 hours of light activity, such as keeping watch. Requires not having Hibernation, which is not selected. This is stronger than the dwarf's separate 60 ft low-light vision.
  • Quadrupedal · level 6: stand from prone for 5 ft, crawl without extra movement cost; prone does not impose disadvantage on melee weapon attacks unless using a two-handed weapon. While prone, reaction to negate incoming melee advantage from prone until end of current turn.
  • Healing Surge · level 9: bonus action, spend a Hit Die, heal its roll. Durable makes minimum HP regained 6; no automatic +INT or +CON is added.

Adapted Resistance · level 9 · choice pending

Choose one: Frightened, Poisoned, Paralyzed, Stunned, Blinded, or Charmed. Gain advantage on saving throws against effects that inflict it and saves to end it; you may replace the choice when gaining a Mutant level. No selection is recorded. Hardy already covers poison saves, but does not choose this feature for you.
