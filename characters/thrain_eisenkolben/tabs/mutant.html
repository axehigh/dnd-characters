Alchemical Nature & Warrior

All Mutant class and secondary mutation properties normally based on Constitution use Intelligence (+4) instead. Mutant save DC: 8 + PB 4 + INT 4 = 16.

Craft (Alchemy) and martial weapon proficiency. Recorded proficiencies: light, medium, heavy armor, shields, simple and martial weapons; Constitution and Charisma saves. Heavy armor comes from the recorded feat, not an extra secondary mutation.

Hit Dice: 9d12. Extra Attack at level 5: two attacks with the Attack action. Total HP and equipped AC have not been supplied.

Mutagens · 4 / long rest

Consume as a bonus action for a one-hour effect. Only one mutagen can be active; consuming another ends the current one.

  • Precision: while active, use a bonus action to focus on a visible target. Weapon attacks against that target crit on 19–20 until the end of your turn. Activation and focusing both use bonus actions; do not assume both in the same turn.
  • Endurance: gain 4 temporary HP at the start of each of your turns in combat. Remaining temporary HP vanish when combat ends; do not add successive grants together.
  • Power: add +4 damage to the first melee weapon attack you hit with on each of your turns.
  • Speed: +10 ft walking speed (40 ft total) and ignore nonmagical difficult terrain.
  • Enhanced Reflexes: +4 to Dexterity checks and saving throws.
  • Enhanced Senses: +4 to Perception and Survival checks.

Alchemical Knight · level 6

3 superiority dice, d6s; expend a die to fuel a maneuver. Regain expended dice when rolling initiative or finishing a short or long rest. Maneuver save DC 16.

Two maneuvers known: Trip Attack and Disarming Attack. Menacing Attack is not recorded as known.

  • Trip Attack: on a weapon hit, spend a superiority die and add it to damage; a Large or smaller target makes a Strength save (DC 16) or falls prone.
  • Disarming Attack: on a weapon hit, spend a superiority die and add it to damage; target makes a Strength save (DC 16) or drops one chosen held item at its feet.

From level 6, ignore Primary Mutation prerequisites when selecting secondary mutations; other prerequisites still apply except for the supplied build's explicit acquisition levels.

Reserves of Strength

  • Steady Arm: reaction when a weapon attack misses; spend a Hit Die and add the roll to the attack, possibly turning it into a hit.
  • Power Through: reaction when hit; spend a Hit Die and subtract the roll from damage before resistances or vulnerabilities.
  • Burst of Power: on a weapon critical hit, spend a Hit Die and add double its roll to damage. No reaction is specified.

All share the 9d12 Hit Dice pool with healing. Expended dice cannot also be spent on short-rest healing; track them until recovered through rest.

Secondary mutations

  • Healing Surge · level 2: bonus action, spend a Hit Die, heal its roll. Durable makes minimum HP regained 4; no automatic +INT or +CON is added.
  • Limit Removal · level 2: with no Hit Dice left, roll a d12 to use a Reserves ability instead and take necrotic damage equal to the roll, ignoring resistances and immunities. Supplied damage rule is used, not the draft's alternate exhaustion result.
  • Quadrupedal · level 6: stand from prone for 5 ft, crawl without extra movement cost; prone does not impose disadvantage on melee weapon attacks unless using a two-handed weapon. While prone, reaction to negate incoming melee advantage from prone until end of current turn.
  • Spines · level 9: creatures grappling you or grappled by you take 1d6 piercing at the start of each of their turns. No magical damage upgrade is recorded.

Adapted Resistance · level 9 · choice pending

Choose one: Frightened, Poisoned, Paralyzed, Stunned, Blinded, or Charmed. Gain advantage on saving throws against effects that inflict it and saves to end it; you may replace the choice when gaining a Mutant level. No selection is recorded. Hardy already covers poison saves, but does not choose this feature for you.
