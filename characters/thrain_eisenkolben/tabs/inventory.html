Armour class 18 with the shield (chain mail 16 + shield 2), or 16 with the shield stowed. Thrain keeps a hand free for alchemical substances, so the shield is carried rather than always strapped: stowing or readying it uses the free object interaction, and throwing acid, fire, fog, or a curative requires the free hand.

The shield can be used as an improvised weapon for 1d4 bludgeoning if needed; it is not a proficient weapon attack. Heavy armour proficiency comes from Heavily Armoured; this suit is the recorded possession, not a bonus granted by the feat.

Movement: chain mail is weighty 13. Strength 14 meets that minimum, so the 10 ft weighty movement penalty does not apply and speed stays 30 ft. The noisy quality gives disadvantage on Stealth checks.

Appearance: dwarven-forged mail worn over a hardened breastplate, with spiked pauldrons, alchemical etching, and a reinforced gauntlet. A round shield bears the same alchemical etching. The suit matches the heavy-armour artwork while using the campaign chain mail statistics.

Attack with the warhammer: +6 to hit (Strength +2, proficiency +4). Damage 1d8+2 one-handed or 1d10+2 with two hands; Fell-handed adds +2 to a successful melee hit, for 1d8+4 or 1d10+4. With the shield readied the warhammer stays one-handed (1d8); stow the shield to use the versatile 1d10 grip. Fury, Power, and Burst of Power are conditional additions described on the sheet.
