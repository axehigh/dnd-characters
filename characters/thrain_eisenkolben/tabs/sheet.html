Features

  • Alchemical Nature & Warrior

    Intelligence replaces Constitution for all Mutant class and secondary mutation DCs and features. Craft (Alchemy) and martial weapon proficiency. This does not replace Constitution for racial traits, feats, or Constitution saves.

  • Mutagens · 3 / long rest

    Bonus action to consume; lasts 1 hour. Only one active: a new mutagen ends the previous one. Precision: bonus action to focus on a visible target; weapon attacks against it crit on 19–20 until end of turn. Endurance: 3 temporary HP at the start of each of your turns in combat, remaining temporary HP vanish when combat ends. Power: +3 damage on first melee weapon hit each of your turns. Speed: +10 ft and ignore nonmagical difficult terrain. Enhanced Reflexes: +3 to Dexterity checks and saves. Enhanced Senses: +3 to Perception and Survival checks.

  • Extra Attack · level 5

    Attack twice instead of once when taking the Attack action.

  • Alchemical Knight · level 6

    3d6 superiority dice, refreshed on initiative or short/long rest; DC 15. Two known maneuvers: Trip Attack and Disarming Attack. Primary Mutation prerequisites for secondary mutations are ignored from level 6; all other prerequisites still apply.

  • Reserves of Strength · 9d12 shared Hit Dice

    Steady Arm: reaction on a weapon miss, spend a Hit Die and add its roll to the attack. Power Through: reaction when hit, spend a Hit Die and subtract its roll from damage before resistance/vulnerability. Burst of Power: on a weapon critical hit, spend a Hit Die and add twice its roll to damage. Spent dice are no longer available for healing.

  • Adapted Resistance · level 9 · Frightened

    Chosen condition: Frightened. Gain advantage on saving throws against effects that inflict Frightened and on saves to end it. You may replace the choice when gaining a Mutant level. This is not immunity or blanket advantage against all six conditions.

  • Spines · secondary mutation · level 2

    Creatures grappling you or grappled by you take 1d6 piercing damage at the start of each of their turns.

  • Nocturnal · secondary mutation · level 2

    Darkvision to 120 ft. Gain the benefits of a long rest with 1 hour asleep and 7 hours of light activity, such as keeping watch. Requires not having Hibernation; Hibernation is not selected. Stronger than the separate dwarf low-light vision trait.

  • Quadrupedal · secondary mutation · level 6

    Stand from prone for 5 ft; crawl without extra movement cost. Prone does not impose disadvantage on melee weapon attacks unless using a two-handed weapon. While prone, use a reaction against an incoming melee attack to negate advantage from prone until end of the current turn.

  • Healing Surge · secondary mutation · level 9

    Bonus action: spend one Hit Die and regain HP equal to the roll (1d12). No automatic Constitution or Intelligence bonus; Durable sets minimum HP regained to 6. Requires Mutant level 3; selected at level 9. Uses the shared Hit Dice pool.

  • Dwarf ability modifiers & Athletics

    +1 Constitution, +1 Wisdom; Athletics proficiency. Already included in the final scores, not increased again.

  • Low-light vision · 60 ft

    Campaign dwarf low-light vision within 60 ft; this racial trait cannot see in complete darkness and is not standard darkvision. Nocturnal separately grants stronger darkvision to 120 ft.

  • Hardy

    Advantage on poison saves and resistance to poison damage.

  • Stonecunning

    History checks about the origin of stonework use double proficiency: INT +3 + twice PB +8 = +11.

  • Tough

    +1 maximum HP per level (+9 at level 9), included in the recorded 124 HP.

  • Resilient · 1 / long rest

    Reaction when taking damage: reduce damage by 1d8 + CON +3. This racial trait still uses Constitution.

  • Dwarf Fortitude · level 1

    +1 Constitution (maximum 20), included in the final score. When you Dodge in combat, spend one Hit Die to heal 1d12 + CON +3. Durable minimum HP regained is 6. Healing consumes a Hit Die; the Dodge action is required.

  • Heavily Armoured · level 1

    Heavy armor proficiency. Proficiency is not ownership of armor; the chain mail and shield listed on the Inventory tab are the recorded gear (AC 18, 16 with the shield stowed).

  • Durable · level 3

    When rolling a Hit Die to regain HP, minimum HP regained equals twice Constitution modifier: 6. Does not set a minimum on attack or damage-reduction rolls.

  • Fury · level 5

    After a creature damages you, gain +2 to attack and damage against that creature until the end of your first round after taking the damage. Not a passive bonus and not against other targets.

  • Fell-handed · level 7

    +half PB (+2) damage on successful melee attacks.

  • Squat Nimbleness · level 9

    Campaign choice: +1 Strength (maximum 20), included in the final score; +5 ft speed (30 ft total), Acrobatics proficiency, advantage on Athletics or Acrobatics checks to escape grapples. The campaign allows Strength or Dexterity, not Intelligence.
