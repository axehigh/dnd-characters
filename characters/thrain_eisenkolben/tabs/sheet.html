Features

  • Alchemical Nature & Warrior

    Intelligence replaces Constitution for all Mutant class and secondary mutation DCs and features. Craft (Alchemy) and martial weapon proficiency. This does not replace Constitution for racial traits, feats, or Constitution saves.

  • Mutagens · 4 / long rest

    Bonus action to consume; lasts 1 hour. Only one active: a new mutagen ends the previous one. Precision: bonus action to focus on a visible target; weapon attacks against it crit on 19–20 until end of turn. Endurance: 4 temporary HP at the start of each combat turn, remaining temporary HP vanish when combat ends. Power: +4 damage on first melee weapon hit each of your turns. Speed: +10 ft and ignore nonmagical difficult terrain. Enhanced Reflexes: +4 to Dexterity checks and saves. Enhanced Senses: +4 to Perception and Survival checks.

  • Extra Attack · level 5

    Attack twice instead of once when taking the Attack action.

  • Alchemical Knight · level 6

    3d6 superiority dice, refreshed on initiative or short/long rest; DC 16. Two known maneuvers: Trip Attack and Disarming Attack. Primary Mutation prerequisites for secondary mutations are ignored from level 6; other prerequisites still apply unless overridden by the supplied build.

  • Reserves of Strength · 9d12 shared Hit Dice

    Steady Arm: reaction on a weapon miss, spend a Hit Die and add its roll to the attack. Power Through: reaction when hit, spend a Hit Die and subtract its roll from damage before resistance/vulnerability. Burst of Power: on a weapon critical hit, spend a Hit Die and add twice its roll to damage. Spent dice are no longer available for healing.

  • Adapted Resistance · level 9 · choice pending

    Choose Frightened, Poisoned, Paralyzed, Stunned, Blinded, or Charmed. Gain advantage on saves against effects inflicting that condition and saves to end it. No condition has been selected; this is not immunity or blanket advantage against all six.

  • Healing Surge · secondary mutation · level 2

    Bonus action: spend one Hit Die and regain HP equal to the roll (1d12). No automatic Constitution or Intelligence bonus; Durable sets minimum HP regained to 4. Uses the shared Hit Dice pool.

  • Limit Removal · secondary mutation · level 2

    When out of Hit Dice, roll a d12 for a Reserves ability instead and take equal necrotic damage. This is costly, not free healing. Uses the supplied build's level and damage rule.

  • Quadrupedal · secondary mutation · level 6

    Stand from prone for 5 ft; crawl without extra movement cost. Prone does not impose disadvantage on melee weapon attacks unless using a two-handed weapon. While prone, use a reaction against an incoming melee attack to negate advantage from prone until end of the current turn.

  • Spines · secondary mutation · level 9

    Creatures grappling you or grappled by you take 1d6 piercing damage at the start of each of their turns.

  • Dwarf ability modifiers & Athletics

    +1 Constitution, +1 Wisdom; Athletics proficiency. Supplied final scores are already recorded, not increased again.

  • Low-light vision · 60 ft

    Campaign low-light vision within 60 ft; cannot see in complete darkness. Not standard darkvision.

  • Hardy

    Advantage on poison saves and resistance to poison damage.

  • Stonecunning

    History checks about the origin of stonework use double proficiency: INT +4 + twice PB +8 = +12.

  • Tough

    +1 maximum HP per level (+9 at level 9). Total HP not supplied.

  • Resilient · 1 / long rest

    Reaction when taking damage: reduce damage by 1d8 + CON +2. This racial trait still uses Constitution.

  • Dwarf Fortitude · level 1

    When you Dodge in combat, spend one Hit Die to heal 1d12 + CON +2. Durable minimum HP regained is 4. Healing consumes a Hit Die; the Dodge action is required.

  • Heavily Armoured · level 1

    Heavy armor proficiency. Proficiency is not ownership of armor; no AC assumed.

  • Durable · level 3

    When rolling a Hit Die to regain HP, minimum HP regained equals twice Constitution modifier: 4. Does not set a minimum on attack or damage-reduction rolls.

  • Old Hatred · level 5

    +PB (+4) melee weapon damage against orcs, goblins, and their kind.

  • Fell-handed · level 7

    +half PB (+2) damage on successful melee attacks.

  • Squat Nimbleness · level 9

    Supplied option: +1 Intelligence or Strength, selected Intelligence; +5 ft speed (30 ft total), Acrobatics proficiency, advantage on Athletics or Acrobatics checks to escape grapples. Uses the supplied Intelligence option, not the draft's Dexterity option.
