Active rules: Campaign dwarf traits and feat rules, not standard dwarf rules. Cultural and general feats may be combined.

Dwarf racial traits

  • Ability modifiers: +1 Constitution and +1 Wisdom. Already included in the final scores; do not add again.
  • Proficiency: Athletics.
  • Low-light vision: 60 ft; campaign text treats dim light as bright light and darkness as dim light, but explicitly does not allow seeing in complete darkness. This racial trait is not standard darkvision. Thrain's Nocturnal secondary mutation separately grants stronger darkvision to 120 ft.
  • Hardy: advantage on poison saves and resistance to poison damage.
  • Stonecunning: History checks on the origin of stonework use double proficiency, even without normal History proficiency: INT +3 + twice PB +8 = +11 here.
  • Tough: +1 maximum HP per level, +9 at level 9, included in the recorded 124 HP.
  • Resilient: reaction when taking damage, reduce it by 1d8 + Constitution modifier (1d8+3), once per long rest. Alchemical Nature does not change this racial trait to Intelligence.

Selected cultural feats

  • Level 1 · Dwarf Fortitude: +1 Constitution (maximum 20), already included. When taking the Dodge action in combat, spend a Hit Die to heal 1d12+3 (Constitution). Consumes a Hit Die; Durable sets minimum HP regained to 6.
  • Level 5 · Fury: after a creature damages you, gain +2 to attack and damage against that creature until the end of your first round after taking the damage. Not passive and not against other targets.
  • Level 9 · Squat Nimbleness: campaign choice of Strength or Dexterity, selected +1 Strength (maximum 20), already included; +5 ft speed for 30 ft total; Acrobatics proficiency; advantage on Athletics or Acrobatics checks to escape grapples.

Selected general feats

  • Level 1 · Heavily Armoured: heavy armor proficiency. The recorded chain mail and shield are possessions listed on the Inventory tab, AC 18 (16 with the shield stowed).
  • Level 3 · Durable: when rolling Hit Dice to regain HP, minimum HP regained is twice CON modifier, 6. Not a minimum attack or damage-reduction roll.
  • Level 7 · Fell-handed: successful melee attacks deal +half PB (+2) damage.

Only campaign feat effects apply. Squat Nimbleness does not increase Intelligence. Ability increases from race and feats are already included in the final scores.
