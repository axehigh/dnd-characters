Active rules: Campaign dwarf traits and the supplied character build, not standard dwarf rules. Cultural and general feats may be combined.

Dwarf racial traits

  • Ability modifiers: +1 Constitution and +1 Wisdom. Already included in the supplied final scores; do not add again.
  • Proficiency: Athletics.
  • Low-light vision: 60 ft; campaign text treats dim light as bright light and darkness as dim light, but explicitly does not allow seeing in complete darkness. Not standard darkvision.
  • Hardy: advantage on poison saves and resistance to poison damage.
  • Stonecunning: History checks on the origin of stonework use double proficiency, even without normal History proficiency: +12 here.
  • Tough: +1 maximum HP per level, +9 at level 9. This is not a recorded HP total.
  • Resilient: reaction when taking damage, reduce it by 1d8 + Constitution modifier (1d8+2), once per long rest. Alchemical Nature does not change this racial trait to Intelligence.

Recorded feat progression

  • Level 1 · Dwarf Fortitude: when taking the Dodge action in combat, spend a Hit Die to heal 1d12+2 (Constitution). Consumes a Hit Die.
  • Level 1 · Heavily Armoured: heavy armor proficiency; no armor or AC automatically granted.
  • Level 3 · Durable: when rolling Hit Dice to regain HP, minimum HP regained is twice CON modifier, 4. Not a minimum attack or damage-reduction roll.
  • Level 5 · Old Hatred: +PB (+4) melee weapon damage against orcs, goblins, and their kind.
  • Level 7 · Fell-handed: successful melee attacks deal +half PB (+2) damage.
  • Level 9 · Squat Nimbleness: supplied +1 Intelligence or Strength option, selected Intelligence; +5 ft speed for 30 ft total; Acrobatics proficiency; advantage on Athletics or Acrobatics checks to escape grapples.

Supplied final scores and feat effects are authoritative. Do not import additional ability increases from generic feat descriptions. The supplied Squat Nimbleness INT option intentionally differs from the campaign draft's STR/DEX wording.
