Combat tactics · preparation and control

  1. Prepare one mutagen: Precision for target-focused 19–20 weapon criticals, or Endurance for 4 temporary HP at the start of each combat turn. Each is a bonus action to consume, lasts 1 hour, and uses one of 4 long-rest uses. A new mutagen ends the old one.
  2. Manage the bonus action: Precision needs a further bonus action to focus a visible target until end of turn. Healing Surge also uses a bonus action. Do not combine these as if all were available in the same turn.
  3. Control the fight: Extra Attack gives two attacks with the Attack action. Trip Attack or Disarming Attack spends one of 3d6 superiority dice on a weapon hit, adds the die to damage, and forces a DC 16 Strength save for its effect. Trip affects Large or smaller targets. Disarm drops a chosen held item. Dice refresh on initiative or a short/long rest.
  4. Defensive turn: Dodge and spend a Hit Die with Dwarf Fortitude to heal 1d12+2 (minimum HP regained 4 with Durable). This is an action plus a Hit Die, not free healing. Bonus-action Healing Surge spends another Hit Die if used; its healing is the die roll, minimum 4.

Choose your reaction

  • Steady Arm: on a weapon miss, spend a Hit Die and add its roll to the attack.
  • Power Through: when hit, spend a Hit Die and reduce damage by its roll before resistance/vulnerability.
  • Resilient: reduce damage by 1d8+2, once per long rest; no Hit Die cost.
  • Quadrupedal: while prone, negate incoming melee advantage due to prone until end of the current turn. Stand for 5 ft or crawl at no extra cost. Prone melee attacks avoid disadvantage unless using a two-handed weapon.

These compete for your reaction. Spending it to turn a miss into a hit means it is unavailable for an incoming hit until refreshed.

Damage and shared resources

Fell-handed adds +2 on successful melee attacks. Old Hatred adds +4 melee weapon damage against orcs, goblins, and their kind. Power, only if active instead of another mutagen, adds +4 to the first melee weapon hit on each of your turns.

On a weapon critical, Burst of Power spends a Hit Die to add twice the roll to damage. Spines deals 1d6 piercing to creatures grappling you or grappled by you at the start of each of their turns.

Reserves and healing all use the same 9d12 Hit Dice pool. With no dice left, Limit Removal uses a replacement d12 and causes necrotic damage equal to the roll, ignoring resistances and immunities. Do not describe that trade as free recovery.

No equipped weapon, weapon damage die, AC, or HP total is assumed. Adapted Resistance remains unselected; do not claim advantage against a chosen condition yet.
