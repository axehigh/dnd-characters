Active rules: Campaign dwarf traits and feat rules, not standard dwarf rules. Cultural and general feats may be combined.

Dwarf racial traits

  • Ability modifiers: +1 Constitution and +1 Wisdom. Already included in the final scores; do not add again.
  • Proficiency: Athletics.
  • Low-light vision: 60 ft; campaign text treats dim light as bright light and darkness as dim light, but explicitly does not allow seeing in complete darkness. This racial trait is not standard darkvision.
  • Hardy: advantage on poison saves and resistance to poison damage.
  • Stonecunning: History checks on the origin of stonework use double proficiency.
  • Tough: +1 maximum HP per level, included in the recorded 91 HP.
  • Resilient: reaction when taking damage, reduce it by 1d8 + Constitution modifier, once per long rest.

Selected cultural feats

  • Level 1 · Squat Nimbleness: +1 Dexterity (maximum 20), already included; +5 ft speed (30 ft total); Acrobatics or Athletics proficiency; advantage on Athletics or Acrobatics checks to escape grapples.
  • Level 5 · Stony step: ignore all difficult terrain.
  • Level 9 · Murmurs of the earth: as a move action, grant yourself a limited form of Tremorsense; while stationary and taking no action, aware of all creatures within 30 ft in contact with the ground. Requires Stony step.

Selected general feats

  • Level 1 · Sharpshooter: ignore long range penalties and half/three-quarters cover; take −5 to attack to add +10 damage.
  • Level 3 · Crossbow Expert: ignore loading; no disadvantage within 5 ft; bonus-action hand crossbow attack.
  • Level 7 · Mobile: speed +10 ft; no opportunity attacks from creatures you attack; Dash ignores difficult terrain.

Only campaign feat effects apply. Ability increases from race and feats are already included in the final scores. Adapted Resistance is locked in as Frightened.
