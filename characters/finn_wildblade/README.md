## Key Traits

- **Personality**: Infectiously charismatic, simply-minded & instinctive, fearless & bold
- **Combat Style**: Acrobatic dual-wielder using Falcon/Serpent/Lion stances
- **Role**: Frontline skirmisher and party face
- **Strengths**: High mobility, devastating crits, strong reactions, great social presence
- **Playstyle**: Act first, think later; protect allies; delegate boring stuff; taunt enemies who underestimate him
