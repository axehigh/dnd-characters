# How to Play Finn Wildblade

A practical roleplaying guide for playing Finn Wildblade (Level 9 Halfling Barbarian, Path of the Beast) consistently.

## Core Philosophy

- **Act first, think later**: Finn lives by gut instinct. If something seems exciting, fun, or righteous, he does it. Long explanations bore him.
- **Heart over brain**: He's warm-hearted and loyal, not cunning. He'll leap into danger without hesitation to protect his friends.
- **Big personality, small stature**: He weaponizes being underestimated. Cheerfulness and bravado are tools—he loves proving people wrong.
- **Earnest to a fault**: Terrible at lying convincingly or scheming. What you see is what you get.

## Voice & Mannerisms

- **Speech**: Energetic, loud, expressive. Use casual phrases, exclamations, bravado. Sometimes takes idioms literally. Keep it short and punchy.
- **Movement**: Constantly fidgeting, bouncing on feet, gesturing wildly. When excited or in combat, whoops and hollers abound.
- **Emotions**: Transparent. Happy = everyone knows. Angry = righteous fury. Cannot hide feelings well.

## Social Encounters

- **The party face**: Lead with charm. Buy rounds, tell wildly embellished stories, secure rooms/lodging with a smile and bluster.
- **Delegate the boring stuff**: Maps, logistics, deciphering texts, careful contract negotiations—look to the studious party members with genuine disinterest.
- **Action over words**: If he makes a promise in the heat of the moment, he'll do his damnedest to keep it, even if it was foolish.
- **Build rapport**: Genuinely curious and accepting. Quick to befriend, slow to hold grudges against those who earn trust back.

## Combat Mindset

- **Aggressive engagement**: Charge in, draw attention, be a disruptive force. Weave between larger foes—dart under legs, flank, harass.
- **Mobile skirmisher**: Use Falcon Stance for rapid strikes. Focus on mobility and multi-attack rounds.
- **Smart reactions**: Save reactions for near-hits (Defensive Duelist, AC boost) or big threats (Second Chance, reroll attack). Coordinate with allies for advantage (Horde Charge).
- **Taunt & intimidate**: Unleash war cries against those who underestimate him. Demoralize foes with bravado.
- **Protector instinct**: Never leave an ally behind if you can reach them. Rage is fueled by protecting those he cares about.

## Roleplaying Tips

- **Embrace impulsivity**: Make the instinctual choice first in tense moments. It's more authentic than the "perfect" tactical play.
- **Keep plans simple**: If someone gives a 5-minute plan, "Right! Let's go smash it!" is the correct response.
- **Use humor**: Laugh at your own mistakes, celebrate wildly on crits, lift spirits after rough fights. He's a lovable goofball.
- **Growth through bonds**: Let wiser friends reel him in when he's going too far. He learns slowly through experience with people he trusts.
- **Don't confuse simple-minded with cruel**: He's good-natured, fiercely loyal, wears his heart on his sleeve.

## Quick Reference

- **Danger**: "HELL YEAH!" then charge.
- **Bureaucracy**: Grimace, delegate to smartest ally.
- **Compliments**: Big grin, flex, tell exaggerated story.
- **Combat mantra**: Hit fast, hit often, be too annoying/small to pin down.
- **Core values**: Freedom, loyalty, protecting the underdog, having a damn good time.
