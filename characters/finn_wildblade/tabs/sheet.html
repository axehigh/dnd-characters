Description & information

Features

  • Rage Class ability

    Bonus action. Resist bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing damage while raging without heavy armor; advantage on Strength checks and saves; melee weapon damage bonus.

  • Ability Modifiers Racial trait

    +1 to Dexterity and Charisma.

  • Proficiencies Racial trait

    One of either Nature, Animal Handling, Medicine, Performance, or Persuasion.

  • Halflings Luck Racial trait

    When you roll a 1 on a d20 for an attack roll, ability check, or saving throw, you can reroll the die and must use the new roll.

  • Brave Racial trait

    You have advantage on saving throws against being frightened.

  • Tiny Racial trait

    You can move through the space of any creature that is of larger size than yours.

  • Fury Racial trait

    When you score a critical hit with a melee attack, you can roll one of the weapon's damage dice one additional time and add it to the extra damage of the critical hit.

  • Full of life Racial trait

    You have resistance to necrotic damage and radiant damage.

  • Vengeance Racial trait

    When you damage a creature with an attack and the creature’s size is larger than yours, you can cause the attack to deal extra damage to the creature. The extra damage equals your level. Once you use this trait, you can’t use it again until you finish a short or long rest.

  • Save Class ability

    Recorded feature referencing saving throw proficiencies (Strength and Constitution) granted by the barbarian class.

  • Unarmored Defense Class ability

    While not wearing armor, your AC equals 10 + Dexterity modifier + Constitution modifier. You can use a shield and still gain this benefit.

  • Extra Attack Class ability

    You can attack twice, instead of once, whenever you take the Attack action on your turn.

  • Fast Movement Class ability

    Your speed increases by 10 feet while you are not wearing heavy armor.

  • Path Feature (Beast - Bestial Strength) Class ability (Subclass)

    Path of the Beast level 6 feature (Bestial Strength): gain swim or climb speed equal to walking speed. Once per turn, extend a jump by the result of a Strength (Athletics) check.

  • Feral Instincts Class ability

    Advantage on initiative rolls. If surprised at the start of combat, you can act normally on your first turn if you enter rage first.

  • Brutal Critical (1 Die) Class ability

    One additional weapon damage die on a melee critical hit at level 9 (+1d4 for a dagger).

  • Piercer General feat

    Feat benefit. Reroll one piercing damage die once per turn; roll an extra weapon damage die on a piercing critical hit (+1d4 for a dagger).

  • Defensive Duelist General feat

    When wielding a finesse weapon with proficiency, use your reaction to add your proficiency bonus to your AC against a melee attack.

  • Squat Nimbleness Cultural feat

    Increase your Strength or Dexterity score by 1 (to a max of 20), increase walking speed by 5 ft, gain proficiency in Acrobatics or Athletics, and have advantage on any Strength (Athletics) or Dexterity (Acrobatics) check to escape from being grappled.

  • Low Blow Cultural feat

    Halfling campaign feat. When attacking a larger creature than yourself, cause extra 1d4 damage per proficiency bonus on any critical hit (+4d4 at proficiency +4).

  • Danger Sense Class ability

    Advantage on Dexterity saving throws against effects that you can see, provided you aren't blinded, deafened, or incapacitated.
