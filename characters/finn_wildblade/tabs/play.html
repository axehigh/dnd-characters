How to Play Finn Wildblade

This guide is designed to help you get into Finn's head and play him consistently in session. It focuses on behavior, instincts, and decision-making.

Core Philosophy

Act First, Think Later

Finn lives by gut instinct. If something seems exciting, fun, or righteous, he does it. Don't over-analyze plans—he gets bored with long explanations. If a plan needs more than a few sentences, his eyes will glaze over.

Heart Over Brain

He's charismatic and warm-hearted, not cunning. He cares deeply for his clan/friends and will leap into danger without hesitation to protect them. His loyalty is fierce and impulsive.

Big Personality, Small Stature

Embrace being underestimated. Finn weaponizes his cheerfulness and bravado—he'll taunt, provoke, or perform to get enemies' attention. He loves proving people wrong when they dismiss him for his size.

Voice & Mannerisms

Speech Style

Energetic, loud, and expressive. Use casual slang, exclamations, and bravado. He takes idioms literally sometimes ("kick 'em where it hurts" becomes very direct). Short sentences work best.

Body Language

Constantly in motion—gesturing wildly, pacing, bouncing on his feet. When excited or in combat, he whoops, hollers, and makes noise. He's not subtle.

Emotional Range

Genuine and transparent. If he's happy, everyone's going to know it. If he's angry, it's a righteous fury. He's terrible at hiding his feelings or lying convincingly.

Social Encounters

The Party Face

Lead with charm and confidence. Buy rounds at taverns, tell ridiculous embellished stories, and get rooms for free with a smile and bluster. Use his high Charisma to smooth things over when possible.

Delegate the Boring Stuff

Maps, logistics, deciphering texts, long negotiations with careful wording—Finn will immediately look to the more studious party members. Play this as genuine disinterest, not rudeness.

Earn Trust Through Action

He'll back up words with action. If he makes a promise in the heat of the moment, he will absolutely try to keep it, even if it was foolish.

Combat Mindset

Engage Aggressively

Charge into the fray and draw attention to yourself. You're the skirmisher that weaves between larger foes. Use terrain and movement to your advantage—dart under legs, flank, and harass.

Pick Your Moments

Use the tactics from the Combat Tactics tab. Go for high-mobility, multi-attack rounds (Falcon Stance) and save reactions for near-hits (Defensive Duelist) or big threats (Second Chance). Coordinate with allies for advantage via Horde Charge.

Taunt & Intimidate

Unleash that terrifying war cry against enemies who underestimate you. Use Intimidation to break enemy morale—Finn thrives on being a disruptive, ferocious force.

Protect Your People

Never abandon an ally in trouble if you can get there. Your rage is fueled by protecting those you care about.

Roleplaying Tips & Pitfalls

  • Embrace impulsivity: Make one instinctual choice per tense moment before thinking of the optimal play. It feels true to Finn.
  • Keep it simple: If the player at the table gives a 5-minute plan, Finn responds with "Right! Let's go smash it!"
  • Avoid scheming: Finn is terrible at deception and long cons. Play him as earnest, even when bluffing—he'll probably oversell it.
  • Use humor: He's a goofball. Laugh at his own mistakes, celebrate wildly on crits, and lift spirits after tough fights.
  • Show growth through bonds: His wisdom comes from his friends. Let them reel him in when he's going too far, and have him learn (slowly) to trust their caution.
  • Don't play dumb cruelly: "Simply minded" doesn't mean mean-spirited. He's good-natured, fiercely loyal, and wears his heart on his sleeve.

Quick Reference

  • Default response to danger: "HELL YEAH!" then charge.
  • Default response to bureaucracy: Grimace, delegate to smartest ally.
  • Default response to compliments: Big grin, flex, tell an exaggerated story.
  • Combat mantra: Hit fast, hit often, be too annoying/small to pin down.
  • Core values: Freedom, loyalty, protecting the underdog, having a damn good time.
