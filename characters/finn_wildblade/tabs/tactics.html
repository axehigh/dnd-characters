◇ Combat Tactics

1. Offensive Tactics & Attack Engine

A. The 4-Attack Falcon Stance Routine

Stance activation: when entering a Rage, select Falcon Stance (Path of the Beast).

Attack breakdown (dual-wielding shortswords or daggers):

  • Main Attack 1 (Attack action): +9 to hit, 1d6 + 5 (DEX) + 2 (Fell-Handed) = 1d6+7 piercing.
  • Main Attack 2 (Extra Attack): +9 to hit, 1d6+7 piercing.
  • Falcon Stance Attack (free, part of the Attack action): +9 to hit, 1d6+7 piercing.
  • Off-hand Strike (bonus action): +9 to hit, 1d6 + 2 (Fell-Handed) = 1d6+2 piercing.

Total turn output: 4d6 + 23 piercing (~37 average DPR if all four strikes land).

B. Overcoming the "Reckless Attack" Limitation

  • The rule: standard Barbarian Reckless Attack requires Strength-based attacks. Since Finn attacks with 20 DEX, he cannot use Reckless Attack.
  • The solution — Horde Charge: coordinate with your melee allies. Whenever an ally moves into melee range with an enemy, you gain advantage on attack rolls against that target until the ally's next turn.

C. The Piercer Damage Reroll

  • Piercer feat: once per turn, when you hit with piercing damage, you can reroll one damage die of your choice.
  • Tactics: if any d6 or d4 in your attack routine rolls a 1 or 2, use Piercer to replace it.

2. The Critical Hit Explosion

When Finn lands a critical hit on a Medium or larger creature with a shortsword, stack the following features:

Critical Hit Damage = 5d6 + 4d4 + 7 piercing.

  • 2d6 — base shortsword die, doubled.
  • +1d6 — Brutal Critical (Barbarian level 9).
  • +1d6 — Halfling Fury (ancestry trait).
  • +1d6 — Piercer feat (general feat).
  • +4d4 — Low Blow (halfling cultural feat: +1d4 per proficiency bonus against larger targets).
  • +7 — flat bonuses (+5 DEX, +2 Fell-Handed).

Average crit damage: ~35 piercing on a single strike.

3. Defensive & Reaction Management

You get one reaction per round — manage it by threat:

  • Near-hits (enemy rolls 16–19 vs your 16 AC): use Defensive Duelist — add your +4 proficiency bonus to AC, raising it to 20 against that attack.
  • Critical hits or high boss rolls (natural 20 or 20+): use Second Chance — force the attacker to reroll the attack die, turning a critical hit into a miss or a normal hit.
  • Escaping grapples & control: Squat Nimbleness grants advantage on Acrobatics checks to escape grapples. With +9 Acrobatics and advantage, enemies can rarely hold you.

4. Frontline Leadership & Intimidation

  • War cry / demoralization: with Intimidation expertise (+10) and 15 Charisma, Finn leads charges with battle cries that demoralize foes.
  • Disarming demeanor: outside of combat, Finn's high Charisma makes him the party's swaggering face for tavern negotiations and rallying allies.
