Active Rules: Campaign draft halfling rules are used over official rules for Finn Wildblade.

Halfling Rules (Campaign Draft)

Active character rules
  • Ability Modifiers Racial trait

    +1 to Dexterity and Charisma.

  • Proficiencies Racial trait

    One of either Nature, Animal Handling, Medicine, Performance, or Persuasion.

  • Halflings Luck Racial trait

    When you roll a 1 on a d20 for an attack roll, ability check, or saving throw, you can reroll the die and must use the new roll.

  • Brave Racial trait

    You have advantage on saving throws against being frightened.

  • Tiny Racial trait

    You can move through the space of any creature that is of larger size than yours.

  • Fury Racial trait

    When you score a critical hit with a melee attack, you can roll one of the weapon's damage dice one additional time and add it to the extra damage of the critical hit.

  • Full of Life Racial trait

    You have resistance to necrotic damage and radiant damage.

  • Vengeance Racial trait

    When you damage a creature with an attack and the creature’s size is larger than yours, you can cause the attack to deal extra damage to the creature. The extra damage equals your level. Once you use this trait, you can’t use it again until you finish a short or long rest.
