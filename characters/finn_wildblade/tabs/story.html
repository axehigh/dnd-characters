Character Story & Description

Finn “Wildblade”

Overview

Finn “Wildblade” is a halfling barbarian whose terrifying presence in battle completely belies his small stature. Vibrant, outgoing, and wildly expressive, Finn relies on an infectious personality and lightning-fast agility rather than brute physical force or intellectual tactics. Outside of combat, he serves as an unlikely, overly confident social face for his adventuring companions. Inside combat, he channels the untamed primal instincts of the wild to leap, dodge, and carve through foes twice his size.

Backstory

Finn was born into a nomadic halfling clan that roamed harsh, monster-ridden borderlands. Growing up in a world dominated by larger, deadlier races, Finn discovered early on that subtle trickery and quiet stealth were not his path. He was drawn instead to the wild spirits of nature and the thrilling surge of primal emotion.

Reading maps, studying ancient history, or grasping long, multi-step plans bored Finn to tears. He experienced the world purely through gut instinct, vibrant emotion, and a disarming smile. What he lacked in formal intellect, he made up for with pure, magnetic charm. Long before he ever raised a blade in anger, Finn was the heartbeat of his clan’s campfire gatherings—telling extravagant stories, charming travelers, and securing hospitality through sheer force of personality.

His life changed forever when a monstrous raiding party ambushed his clan’s temporary settlement. While others hesitated to formulate a defense, Finn acted entirely on raw instinct. Letting out a bloodcurdling battle cry that rattled the armor of the attackers, he darted into the fray in a whirlwind of reckless fury. Moving faster than the eye could follow, he wove under heavy swings and struck down the raiding leader. He earned the title “Wildblade” that day, proving that true fury comes from the spirit, not physical size.

Realizing that his home borderlands were only a tiny corner of a vast world, Finn set out on the road as an adventurer and mercenary. He knows he isn't the companion to consult for deciphering ancient ruins, solving complex riddles, or navigating intricate overland routes. Instead, he relies on his wiser and more studious allies to handle the logistics while he takes care of the talking, the drinking, and the frontline fighting.

Personality & Mannerisms

Infectiously Charismatic

Loud, friendly, and utterly unbothered by social anxiety, Finn walks into any room as if he owns it. He disarms strangers with a wide grin, easily secures lodging, and wins over skeptical crowds through pure flair.

Simply Minded & Instinctive

Long explanations lose his interest immediately. He takes idioms literally, misses subtle social cues, and trusts his immediate gut feelings over deliberate analysis.

Fearless & Bold

Coupled with his natural halfling bravery, Finn's lack of tactical overthinking means he rarely pauses to calculate the odds. He throws himself into dangerous situations with absolute, unhesitating confidence.

Combat Style & Bestial Stances

Finn fights with fluid, acrobatic graceful intensity rather than heavy, lumbering blows. Shedding bulky armor to rely on swift movement and instinctual reflexes, he weaves between the legs of giant opponents and strikes from unexpected angles. When his primal rage takes hold, he channels bestial stances that adapt to the flow of battle:

Falcon Stance

Finn becomes a swift, relentless hunter, raining down a lightning-fast flurry of rapid strikes with lighter blades.

Serpent Stance

Swaying like a venomous snake, Finn uses his weapon to parry incoming blows in a split second, turning fatal attacks aside.

Lion Stance

Channeling an apex predator, Finn feeds on the adrenaline of battle, shrugging off severe injuries with stubborn vitality.

Whenever a foe underestimates Finn because of his small size, the halfling weaponizes his sheer force of personality—releasing a terrifying, echoic war cry that leaves enemies unnerved and shaken to their core.
