Barbarian Core & Proficiencies

Hit Points: 1d12 + Constitution modifier per Barbarian level

Armor: Light armor, medium armor, shields

Weapons: Simple weapons, martial weapons

Saving Throws: Strength, Constitution

Skills: Choose two from Animal Handling, Athletics, Intimidation, Nature, Perception, and Survival.

Equipment: (a) a greataxe or (b) any martial melee weapon; (a) two handaxes or (b) any simple weapon; Adventure pack.

Class Features (Level 1–9 Achieved, Levels 10–20 Faded)

  • Level 1 · Rage Class ability

    Bonus action. Resist bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing damage while raging without heavy armor; advantage on Strength checks and saves; melee weapon damage bonus.

  • Level 1 · Unarmored Defense Class ability

    While not wearing armor, your AC equals 10 + Dexterity modifier + Constitution modifier. You can use a shield and still gain this benefit.

  • Level 2 · Reckless Attack Class ability

    Advantage on Strength-based melee weapon attacks; attacks against you gain advantage until your next turn.

  • Level 2 · Danger Sense Class ability

    Advantage on Dexterity saving throws against effects you can see, provided you aren't blinded, deafened, or incapacitated.

  • Level 3 · Primal Path Class ability

    Choose a path at 3rd level that shapes your rage (e.g., Berserker or Beast), granting features at 3rd, 6th, 10th, and 14th levels.

  • Level 4 · Ability Score Improvement Class ability

    Increase one ability score by 2 or two by 1 at levels 4, 8, 12, 16, and 19 (max 20).

  • Level 5 · Extra Attack Class ability

    Attack twice instead of once whenever you take the Attack action.

  • Level 5 · Fast Movement Class ability

    Speed increases by 10 feet while not wearing heavy armor.

  • Level 6 · Path Feature (Bestial Strength) Class ability (Subclass)

    Gain swim or climb speed equal to walking speed. Once per turn, extend a jump by the result of a Strength (Athletics) check.

  • Level 7 · Feral Instinct Class ability

    Advantage on initiative; act normally on first turn when surprised if you enter rage first.

  • Level 8 · Ability Score Improvement Class ability

    Increase one ability score by 2 or two by 1 (max 20).

  • Level 9 · Brutal Critical (1 die) Class ability

    Roll additional weapon damage dice on melee critical hits (+1 die at 9th).

  • Level 10 · Path Feature (Furious Hunter) Class ability (Subclass)

    Path of the Beast level 10 feature (Furious Hunter): when hitting a creature while raging, impose an effect (Hound, Maim, or Maul). Usable proficiency bonus times per long rest.

  • Level 11 · Relentless Rage Class ability

    Drop to 1 hp instead of 0 when raging on a DC 10 Constitution save (DC increases by 5 each time).

  • Level 12 · Ability Score Improvement Class ability

    Increase one ability score by 2 or two by 1 (max 20).

  • Level 13 · Brutal Critical (2 dice) Class ability

    Two additional weapon damage dice on melee critical hits.

  • Level 14 · Path Feature (Call the Hunt) Class ability (Subclass)

    Path of the Beast level 14 feature (Call the Hunt): when entering rage, choose willing creatures within 30 feet. Gain 5 temp HP per creature and grant a d6 damage bonus once per turn.

  • Level 15 · Persistent Rage Class ability

    Your rage ends early only if you fall unconscious or choose to end it.

  • Level 16 · Ability Score Improvement Class ability

    Increase one ability score by 2 or two by 1 (max 20).

  • Level 17 · Brutal Critical (3 dice) Class ability

    Three additional weapon damage dice on melee critical hits.

  • Level 18 · Indomitable Might Class ability

    If your Strength check total is less than your Strength score, use that score instead.

  • Level 19 · Ability Score Improvement Class ability

    Increase one ability score by 2 or two by 1 (max 20).

  • Level 20 · Primal Champion Class ability

    Strength and Constitution increase by 4 (max 24).

Primal Paths

Path of the Berserker

  • Level 3 · Frenzy Class ability (Subclass)

    Go into a frenzy when you rage, allowing a single melee weapon attack as a bonus action on each turn. Gain one level of exhaustion when rage ends.

  • Level 6 · Mindless Rage Class ability (Subclass)

    You can't be charmed or frightened while raging.

  • Level 10 · Intimidating Presence Class ability (Subclass)

    Use an action to frighten a creature within 30 feet using a Wisdom saving throw (DC 8 + proficiency + Charisma modifier).

  • Level 14 · Retaliation Class ability (Subclass)

    Use your reaction to make a melee weapon attack against a creature within 5 feet that damages you.

Path of the Beast

  • Level 3 · Stance of the Beast Class ability (Subclass)

    Assume a stance upon entering rage: Lion (gain temporary HP equal to proficiency once per turn when hitting if below half HP), Falcon (make one additional attack with a light melee weapon once per turn when attacking), or Serpent (use reaction to roll a d8 to add to AC against a melee attack within reach).

  • Level 6 · Bestial Strength Class ability (Subclass)

    Gain swim or climb speed equal to walking speed. Once per turn, extend a jump by the result of a Strength (Athletics) check.

  • Level 10 · Furious Hunter Class ability (Subclass)

    When hitting a creature while raging, impose an effect: Hound (forced movement away), Maim (disadvantage on attack rolls), or Maul (extra 2d12 damage). Usable a number of times equal to proficiency bonus per long rest.

  • Level 14 · Call the Hunt Class ability (Subclass)

    When entering rage, choose willing creatures within 30 feet (up to your Constitution modifier). Gain 5 temp HP per creature. Chosen creatures can add a d6 to damage once per turn.
