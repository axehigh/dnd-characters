Barbarian Core & Proficiencies

Hit Points: 1d12 + Constitution modifier per Barbarian level

Armor: Light armor, medium armor, shields

Weapons: Simple weapons, martial weapons

Saving Throws: Strength, Constitution

Skills: Choose two from Animal Handling, Athletics, Intimidation, Nature, Perception, and Survival.

Equipment: (a) a greataxe or (b) any martial melee weapon; (a) two handaxes or (b) any simple weapon; Adventure pack.

Class Features (Level 1–9 Achieved, Levels 10–20 Faded)

  • Level 1 · Rage Class ability

    Enter a rage as a bonus action. While raging without heavy armor: advantage on Strength checks and Strength saving throws; a bonus to melee weapon damage rolls using Strength (see table); resistance to bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing damage. Rage lasts 1 minute and ends early if knocked unconscious, if your turn ends without attacking a hostile creature or taking damage, or if you end it as a bonus action. Rages refresh on a long rest.

  • Level 1 · Unarmored Defense Class ability

    While not wearing armor, your AC equals 10 + Dexterity modifier + Constitution modifier. You can use a shield and still gain this benefit.

  • Level 2 · Reckless Attack Class ability

    When you make your first attack on your turn, you can attack recklessly: advantage on Strength-based melee weapon attack rolls this turn, but attack rolls against you have advantage until your next turn.

  • Level 2 · Danger Sense Class ability

    Advantage on Dexterity saving throws against effects you can see, provided you aren't blinded, deafened, or incapacitated.

  • Level 3 · Primal Path (Path of the Beast) Class ability

    Choose a path that shapes your rage. You follow the Path of the Beast, which grants features at 3rd, 6th, 10th, and 14th levels (detailed below).

  • Level 5 · Extra Attack Class ability

    Attack twice instead of once whenever you take the Attack action.

  • Level 5 · Fast Movement Class ability

    Speed increases by 10 feet while not wearing heavy armor.

  • Level 6 · Path Feature — Bestial Strength Class ability (Subclass)

    Gain a swim or climb speed equal to your walking speed, and extend a jump by a Strength (Athletics) check once per turn. Full rules: see Path of the Beast, below.

  • Level 7 · Feral Instinct Class ability

    Advantage on initiative rolls. If surprised at the start of combat and not incapacitated, you can act normally on your first turn, but only if you enter your rage first.

  • Level 9 · Brutal Critical (1 die) Class ability

    Roll one additional weapon damage die when determining the extra damage for a melee critical hit (two at 13th, three at 17th).

  • Level 10 · Path Feature — Furious Hunter Class ability (Subclass)

    When you hit a creature with a melee weapon attack while raging, impose a bestial effect (Hound, Maim, or Maul). Usable proficiency bonus times per long rest. Full rules: see Path of the Beast, below.

  • Level 11 · Relentless Rage Class ability

    If you drop to 0 hit points while raging and don't die outright, make a DC 10 Constitution save to drop to 1 hit point instead. The DC increases by 5 each time, resetting on a short or long rest.

  • Level 13 · Brutal Critical (2 dice) Class ability

    Two additional weapon damage dice on melee critical hits.

  • Level 14 · Path Feature — Call the Hunt Class ability (Subclass)

    When you enter your rage, choose allies to join your hunt, gaining temporary hit points and granting a damage bonus. Full rules: see Path of the Beast, below.

  • Level 15 · Persistent Rage Class ability

    Your rage ends early only if you fall unconscious or choose to end it.

  • Level 17 · Brutal Critical (3 dice) Class ability

    Three additional weapon damage dice on melee critical hits.

  • Level 18 · Indomitable Might Class ability

    If your total for a Strength check is less than your Strength score, you can use that score in place of the total.

  • Level 20 · Primal Champion Class ability

    Your Strength and Constitution scores increase by 4, and your maximum for those scores becomes 24.

Path of the Beast

Your Primal Path. Several features replace standard path features, as noted in the campaign draft.

  • Level 3 · Stance of the Beast Class ability (Subclass)

    Replaces the Form of the Beast feature. Starting when you choose this path at 3rd level, when you enter your rage, you can assume one of the fighting stances listed below for the duration of your rage. You choose the stance you take each time you rage:

    • Lion Stance. Once on each of your turns when you damage a creature with a melee weapon, you gain temporary hit points equal to your proficiency bonus, provided you have less than half your hit points when you hit.
    • Falcon Stance. Once on each of your turns when you attack with a melee weapon with the light property using the Attack action, you can make one additional attack with that weapon as part of the same action.
    • Serpent Stance. If a creature you can see within reach of a melee weapon you are wielding hits you with an attack roll, you can use your reaction to swipe your weapon and roll a d8, applying a bonus to your AC equal to the number rolled, potentially causing the attack to miss you.
  • Level 6 · Bestial Strength Class ability (Subclass)

    Replaces the Bestial Soul feature. You gain a swim speed or a climb speed equal to your walking speed (your choice). In addition, when you jump, you can make a Strength (Athletics) check and extend your jump by a number of feet equal to the check's total. You can make this special check only once per turn.

  • Level 10 · Furious Hunter Class ability (Subclass)

    Replaces the Infectious Fury feature. When you hit a creature with a melee weapon attack while raging, you can pursue the target with bestial fury, imposing one of the following effects of your choice. If an effect causes a saving throw, the DC is 8 + your Constitution modifier + your proficiency bonus.

    • Hound. The target must make a Wisdom saving throw. On a failed save, it must immediately use its reaction, if available, to move as far as its speed allows away from you. It doesn't move into obviously dangerous ground, such as a fire or a pit. A creature that cannot be frightened is immune to this effect.
    • Maim. The target must make a Constitution saving throw. On a failed save, it has disadvantage on attack rolls until the start of your next turn.
    • Maul. The target must make a Dexterity saving throw, taking 2d12 damage of your weapon's type on a failure.

    You can use this feature a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a long rest.

  • Level 14 · Call the Hunt Class ability (Subclass)

    When you enter your rage, you can choose a number of other willing creatures you can see within 30 feet of you equal to your Constitution modifier (minimum of one creature). You gain 5 temporary hit points for each creature that accepts this feature. Until the rage ends, the chosen creatures can use the following benefit once on each of their turns: when the creature hits a target with an attack roll and deals damage to it, the creature can roll a d6 and gain a bonus to the damage equal to the number rolled.

    You can use this feature a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a long rest.
