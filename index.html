Class rules reference

Standard 2014 reference · descriptions may differ from campaign rules

Rage

Bonus action. Resist bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing damage while raging without heavy armor.

Unarmored Defense

AC = 10 + Dexterity + Constitution when not wearing armor; a shield is allowed.

Reckless Attack

Advantage on Strength-based melee weapon attacks; attacks against you gain advantage until your next turn.

Danger Sense

Advantage on Dexterity saves against effects you can see, subject to condition restrictions.

Extra Attack

Attack twice when you take the Attack action.

Fast Movement

+10 ft speed while not wearing heavy armor.

Feral Instinct

Advantage on initiative; rage can let you act when surprised, with restrictions.

Brutal Critical

One additional weapon damage die on a melee critical hit at level 9 (+1d4 for a dagger).

Official · Path of the Beast

Tasha’s Cauldron of Everything
Level 3 · Form of the Beast

When raging, choose a bite, claws, or tail as a natural weapon. These use Strength, so Dexterity 18 does not automatically grant a +4 attack or damage bonus.

The bite can restore HP while below half health; claws can grant an additional claw attack; the tail offers reach and a reaction to increase AC against a triggering attack. See the published rules for exact conditions.

Level 6 · Bestial Soul

The natural weapons count as magical for overcoming resistance and immunity to nonmagical attacks and damage. After a short or long rest, choose a swimming, climbing, or enhanced-jumping adaptation.

The level 10 and 14 features are not available to a level 9 barbarian. Optional class features are not assumed.

Local · Path of the Beast

Campaign draft · unconfirmed for Finn
Level 3 · Stance of the Beast

Replaces Form of the Beast. Choose a stance each time you enter rage.

Lion: once on each of your turns, a melee weapon hit grants temporary HP equal to proficiency if you have less than half your HP.

Falcon: once on each of your turns, attacking with a light melee weapon using the Attack action allows one additional attack with that weapon in the same action.

Serpent: when a visible creature within your melee weapon’s reach hits you, use a reaction to add 1d8 to AC against that attack, potentially turning it into a miss.

Level 6 · Bestial Strength

Replaces Bestial Soul. Choose a swim or climb speed equal to walking speed. Once per turn, a Strength (Athletics) check can extend a jump by the check’s total in feet.

Standard 2014 halflings are Small, have a 25 ft base walking speed, and have the following ancestry traits:

Lucky

Reroll a 1 on a d20 attack roll, ability check, or saving throw; use the new roll.

Brave

Advantage on saving throws against being frightened.

Halfling Nimbleness

Move through the space of a creature larger than you.
